# Super Sejong Engine — J: Lexicon, Collocations, Redundancy, and Translationese

**Research status:** 2026-10-03. This document proposes a conservative, suggestion-only lexical layer. The cited URLs were opened and inspected. No restricted corpus was downloaded, no API credential was requested, no quantitative experiment was run, and no product code was changed. Papers for which only an abstract was accessible are marked accordingly.

## Main finding

Natural Korean expression depends on the **sense and grammatical relation of words in context**, not a blacklist of repeated roots or a global preference for shorter phrases. The National Institute of Korean Language (NIKL) Korean Basic Dictionary explicitly includes expressions such as **영향을 미치다**, **결론을 내리다**, and **관심을 가지다** in its examples or usage patterns. Their presence is evidence against marking the phrases inherently erroneous; it does not guarantee that every use is natural. Conversely, NIKL calls **과반수 이상** structurally redundant but stops short of calling every occurrence wrong. In an administrative threshold, **과반수** means more than half, whereas **반수 이상** includes exactly half. The 2021 report itself gives **과반수 참석 여부 확인** in one revision of an example and **반수 이상 참석 확인** in a later revision that explains the intended threshold includes half. That inconsistency makes a fixed replacement particularly unsafe. [Dictionary: 미치다](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=70860); [dictionary: 결론](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=15966&nation=eng); [dictionary: 관심](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=60096&nation=eng); [NIKL threshold explanation](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=316366); [NIKL public-language guide, PDF pages 59–60 and 379](https://www.korean.go.kr/common/download.do?c_file_name=4b822627-0996-4319-abe8-833b1248da4e.pdf&file_path=reportData&o_file_name=%ED%96%89%EC%A0%95%EB%AC%B8%EC%84%9C+%ED%91%9C%ED%98%84+%EA%B0%9C%EC%84%A0+%EB%B0%8F+%EC%89%AC%EC%9A%B4+%EA%B3%B5%EA%B3%B5%EC%96%B8%EC%96%B4+%EC%93%B0%EA%B8%B0+%EC%A7%80%EC%B9%A8+%EA%B0%9C%EB%B0%9C%282021%29.pdf).

The product implication is a **contextual diagnostic**: retrieve lexical evidence, recognize a candidate's sense/argument structure and the document's genre, show one optional edit with its reason, and decline to suggest when evidence or meaning is uncertain. A dictionary example is evidence of attested, acceptable usage; it is **not** proof that every syntactic frame is appropriate, or that non-listed expressions are wrong.

## Why Korean collocations need more than string matching

The full [Kim et al. 1999 paper](https://aclanthology.org/W99-0610.pdf) models relations at the morpheme level because a Korean *eojeol* combines content and function morphemes, and arguments can move away from predicates. Its interrupted-bigram design is direct evidence against an adjacent-token-only engine for Korean lexical pairing. The paper's manually assessed 86.3% precision for extracted bigrams and 92% for longer collocations do **not** transfer to this product: its precision measured the plausibility of extracted collocations in the Yonsei balanced corpus, without modern, genre-stratified rewrite judgments or a reported recall estimate. The paper itself notes evaluation difficulty without an existing collocation database. [Kim et al. 1999, pp. 1–3 and 8–9](https://aclanthology.org/W99-0610.pdf).

An implementable representation is a lemma- and sense-aware relation tuple, e.g. `(governor lemma, dependent lemma, dependency/particle, sense, genre, source)`, with the original surface realization retained. This can represent **결론을 내리다** despite verb inflection and intervening modifiers. A corpus association statistic can rank tuples for *human review*, but no one statistic is a proof of correctness: the [2016 Korean spoken-corpus experiment](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002081450) found different leading measures by pair type. Thus score at least observed count, association strength, grammatical relation, sense consistency, and genre support separately. Do not use a low-frequency penalty to ban creative but grammatical combinations, or use high frequency alone to certify a phrase.

The dictionary's [Open API specification](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/kor/openApi/openApiInfo) exposes definitions, usage patterns and examples. Its search response provides a headword `target_code` and `sense_order`; the documented view response describes `sense_info` but does not promise a separate stable sense ID. A lexical layer should therefore record the headword code, sense position or a locally assigned sense key, and the source version, then verify these mappings when dictionary data changes. **결론** has a section-of-text sense and a judgment/decision sense; **결론을 내리다** belongs to the latter. The listed synonyms **맺음말** and **마무리** attach to the section-of-text sense, so a headword-wide synonym swap would conflate the two. Likewise **관심을 가지다** and **영향을 미치다** are listed usage patterns and are not inherently “AI-sounding.” [Dictionary: 결론](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=15966&nation=eng); [dictionary: 관심](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=60096&nation=eng); [dictionary: 미치다](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/eng/dicSearch/SearchView?ParaWordNo=70860).

## A conservative suggestion-only lexicon

**Data unit.** Each rule should record `trigger`, `scope` (genre/register), `required morphology`, `required semantic sense`, `excluded contexts`, `suggestions`, `reason`, `source URL/version`, and `review confidence`. Provenance must distinguish (a) modern normative rule, (b) dictionary attestation, (c) corpus distribution, and (d) editorial style preference. An exact-match trigger is only a candidate generator; it cannot authorize a replacement.

**Processing sequence.** Parse the sentence into morphemes, lemmas and grammatical relations; identify quotations, titles, proper names, technical terms, numbers and legal thresholds; select a sense; retrieve curated dictionary and contextual evidence; propose at most one or two edits with an explanation; then reject any candidate that changes quantities, modality, polarity, agency, named entities, or a protected term. If the sense or dependency parse is uncertain, output no suggestion. Never silently mutate source text.

This table is a design proposal, not a production-ready lexical database. In particular, the NIKL public-language guide is a **genre-specific editorial source**; it cannot override the author's legitimate technical usage or the semantics of a statute. For translationese, the reviewer should record the *mechanism* (e.g. misplaced modifier, excessive passive, calqued punctuation), the intended meaning, and the genre rather than assigning a blanket “foreign” label. NIKL's 2019 diagnostic report separates these mechanisms, and the 2012 essay shows why origin-only detection is unsound. [NIKL 2019 report](https://www.korean.go.kr/common/download.do?c_file_name=a35ef634-52dc-47d6-9d86-0c9795dd8d33_0.pdf&file_path=reportData&o_file_name=2019%EB%85%84+%EC%A4%91%EC%95%99%ED%96%89%EC%A0%95%EA%B8%B0%EA%B4%80+%EA%B3%B5%EA%B3%B5%EC%96%B8%EC%96%B4+%EC%A7%84%EB%8B%A8+%EC%B5%9C%EC%A2%85+%EB%B3%B4%EA%B3%A0%EC%84%9C.pdf); [Kim Han-sik 2012](https://www.korean.go.kr/nkview/nklife/2012_1/22_0106.pdf).

## Licensing and data-acquisition decision

The [Korean Basic Dictionary copyright page](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/kor/kboardPolicy/copyRightTermsInfo) explicitly allows commercial use of most text under CC BY-SA 2.0 KR, subject to attribution and share-alike for adaptations. The [API page](https://krdict.korean.go.kr/kor/openApi/openApiInfo) requires an issued key. Before storing/exporting a derived lexical list, document exact item provenance, third-party exceptions, attribution placement, share-alike obligations and whether a database export counts as an adaptation. This is an implementation/legal review point, not a claim that a custom product's entire code automatically acquires the same license.

The [*모두의 말뭉치* FAQ](https://kli.korean.go.kr/corpus/boards/faqList.do) is materially different: download and use are governed by an approved purpose, duration and agreement; external release of derived results needs advance NIKL approval; raw corpus passages cannot appear in those results; some subcorpora are research-only. Accordingly, **do not build a shipped collocation table from this corpus or the 21st Century Sejong Project by default**. A corpus-based research experiment would first need the relevant access/terms and approval. Do not mistake a published paper's use of the Sejong corpus for a license to reuse that corpus or its extracted 5,820-item list. [Lin thesis repository abstract](https://dcollection.korea.ac.kr/srch/srchDetail/000000127826).

## Falsifiable evaluation plan (not yet executed)

1. Curate ~200 short passages across public information, informal dialogue, marketing, academic prose, legal/administrative thresholds and creative prose. Include author-approved “leave unchanged” examples, especially dictionary-attested collocations and intentional repetition. License or author each passage separately.
2. Have two trained native Korean editors independently label: exact span; lexical sense; whether the phrase is erroneous, merely awkward, or acceptable; whether the proposed edit preserves proposition, threshold, polarity, register and emphasis. Adjudicate disagreements and retain them as ambiguity cases rather than forcing a single gold answer.
3. Compare baseline string-list triggers with morphology/dependency/sense/genre guards. Report **precision of shown suggestions**, false-positive rate on acceptable collocations, meaning-change rate, abstention rate, and results by genre; report inter-annotator agreement. A high abstention rate is acceptable initially if it protects meaning.
4. For any licensed corpus analysis, perform ablations of count-only, PMI, log-likelihood and relation-conditioned scoring on a held-out, human-adjudicated set. The 2016 spoken-corpus finding warns against declaring one association measure universally best. [Lee 2016 abstract](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002081450).
5. Only after meaning-safety and false-positive thresholds are met should the engine use lexical suggestions in candidate ranking. Even then, the lexicon should not automatically replace the author's prose.

The unanswered empirical question is whether these context-aware suggestions improve judged **naturalness while preserving meaning** in this service's actual user genres. None of the opened collocation-extraction studies directly tested that product outcome.
