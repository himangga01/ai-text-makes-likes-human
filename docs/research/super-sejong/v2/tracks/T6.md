### 1c. Consequences for v2

- **Voice reference.** "Keep the writer's voice" for recent posts means keeping the AI draft's published form. Idempotence matters more than improvement.
- **Detector and gate evidence is on AI-formula text.** Blog precision/recall numbers hold on that text. They are not evidence about the owner's own drafts or other bloggers.
- **Human controls.** News and KatFish human text are the controls, but they contain almost no CTAs. CTA behaviour on human CTA-rich prose is untested (open question).
- **Idempotence design.**
  - Pass 1, deterministic: `safe_repair` fixes spacing, `-ㄹ께요` and `-시-ㄹ게요`. It is idempotent on all 3,909 corpus docs, and `compare(x, safe_repair(x))` has 0 hard and 0 soft findings.
  - Pass 2, LLM: pass the CTA ledger as a verbatim-protected block, with "copy unchanged" as the default. The model may touch only a sentence that carries a listed wording finding.
  - Backstop: `strict_identity(src, rw)` returns CTA sentences that were edited. Edited and not equal to `safe_repair(sentence)` means reject.
  - Cost of strict mode [M]: it rejects 72 of 91 ledger-preserving constructed pairs (79%). That is acceptable for already-processed text, too strict for fresh drafts.
  - Accepted by strict mode: all 6 spacing-repair pairs, and 0 of 17 CTA-free sources were falsely flagged.
  - Check on the owner's text: `wording_findings` found 0 defects in the 10 drafts and 3 defective spacing spots in the 20 posts (3 of 144 CTA units, 2.1%). A re-run should leave more than 97% of the owner's CTA sentences byte-identical.
  - I cannot test the LLM step (CLIs are off limits). This is the design plus the deterministic parts only.

## 3. CTA components and what v2 must track

| Component | What it is | Why it matters [R] | Detector support [M] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Action verb | The verb or verbal noun (확인, 문의, 제출). | The CTA itself. Closed-class acts such as 문의/구독/공감 have no near-synonyms. | Yes |
| Object | The `을/를` noun. | An object swap changes the CTA. | Yes, soft |
| Force | Imperative, request, trial, advisory, option. | Weakening or strengthening is the main failure. | Yes (ladder below) |
| Intensifier | `꼭`, `반드시`, `지금 바로`. | Changes strength by one level. | Yes |
| Polarity | Do versus don't. | Flipping reverses the CTA. | Yes |
| Condition | `~하시면`, `필요하시다면`. | Dropping it broadens who is asked. | Yes |
| Deadline | `4월 30일까지`, `마감 전`. | A date changed by one digit is a real error. | Yes |
| Channel | Phone, URL, handle, 카톡, 링크. | Exactness matters for contact CTAs. | Yes |
| Benefit | `무료`, `혜택`. | Part of specificity. | Yes |
| Single versus multiple actions | `문의하고 신청하세요`. | Dropping one action loses content. | Yes |
| Placement | Heading, middle, closing. | Moving the closing CTA changes flow. | Partly (soft `CTA_MOVED`) |
| Tone fit | Speech level, honorific, proportion of pressure. | Handled by the rubric, plus the 말투 track. | Level and honorific only |

## 4. Inventory of Korean CTA forms and the force ladder

The ladder is my design, not an established scale. Counts are from the owner's 20 posts, 144 units, by detector form. The 10 drafts have 107.

| Force | Class | Forms (examples) | Posts (144) | Drafts (107) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Emphatic | directive plus necessity/immediacy adverb (`꼭 확인하세요`, `반드시 ~하십시오`, `지금 바로 신청하세요`) | within imperative | 0 |
| 4 | Directive | imperative `~하세요/하십시오/하라`; request `~해 주세요/주시기 바랍니다/부탁드립니다/주시면 감사하겠습니다`; prohibition `~하지 마세요` | imp 62, request 11, prohibitive 13 | 74 / 5 / 8 |
| 3 | Softened | trial `~해 보세요`; propositive `~합시다/하시죠/해 볼까요`; conditional offer `연락 주시면 안내해 드립니다`; conditional wish `~해 주시면 좋겠습니다` | trial 16, suggestion 2, offer 1 | 8 / 0 / 4 |
| 2 | Advisory | `~하는 것이 좋습니다`; `권합니다/추천합니다`; `~는 건 어떨까요?`; invitation `환영합니다`; instruction `~하시면 됩니다` | advisory 37, instruction 1 | 7 / 1 |
| 1 | Option | `~해 보는 것도 좋습니다`, `~하셔도 됩니다` | 1 | 0 |
| 0 | Not CTA | greetings and blessings, commissives (`정리해 보겠습니다`, `안내해 드립니다`), ability statements, plain deontic statements | n/a | n/a |

Decisions and boundaries:
- **Plain deontic statements** (`~해야 합니다`, `~가 필요합니다`) are not CTAs. They are tracked separately. [M] They are the most frequent directive-adjacent form: about 177–195 per 1,000 sentences in the posts, and 95 of 640 sentences labelled in the drafts. The detector flags none of them. The existing guard (`DEONT OBLIG`) already tracks them. Converting an imperative into a deontic statement is flagged as soft `CTA_TO_DEONTIC` for task-tier CTAs, and as a hard drop for contact and engagement CTAs.
- **Reader-guidance 청유 forms** (`함께 살펴볼까요`, `생각해 보자`) count as CTA forms but are labelled borderline (b=1) in the data, because they guide reading rather than ask for a real-world action.
- **Wish-as-advice** (`~했으면 좋겠습니다`, no `-시-`) is not detected, on purpose. It fires on opinion statements ("I wish science would develop").
- **Not handled:**
  - `-지 말고` inside a sentence ending in a deontic
  - declarative procedural bullets (`확인합니다`, `준비합니다`), a large template in the Hermes drafts (e.g. D229, D236, D254 style lines)
  - dialogue and transcript requests
  - the `-았으면 해요` wish form

### Detector design (`T6/cta_detect.py`)

- Python 3.9+, standard library only, regex only. Worst case 0.52 s on adversarial input.
- Speed on 38.9k news sentences: about 2 s.
- Units are sentences, line-unit headings, and button lines.
- Cue families are tested at sentence end, first match wins, after masking quoted spans (an unclosed opening quote masks to the end).
- Exclusions: greetings and blessings, quotations and reported speech, `wh-` questions, system or academic subjects (`이 논문은 … 제안합니다`), society wishes, dialogue-transcript markers, mental-verb `보시면 됩니다`.
- Run-ons longer than 400 chars are analysed by their last 400 chars.
- Extraction fields: form, force, tier, polarity, speech level, honorific, action(s), object, condition, timing, deadline, channel, benefit, intensity, modifiers, heading flag, paragraph index.
- Example ledger entry (a constructed paragraph):
  - `{"text":"궁금한 점이 있으시면 02-123-4567로 전화해 주세요.","form":"request","force":4,"tier":"contact","level":"haeyo","action":"전화","condition":"궁금한 점이 있으시면","channel":["02-123-4567","전화"],"deadline":[],"benefit":[],"intensity":[]}`

| `N해주세요` (N is 2+ syllables) → `N해 주세요` | norm violation | [S] NIKL 331217, 326874 | `[가-힣]{2,}해주세요/주시기/…` | `이렇게 해주세요` (permitted) | low | all | 1/20 posts, 2/2,740 news docs |

| `할께요` → `할게요` | spelling | [S] NIKL 308180 | `할/갈/볼/줄/올/드릴께요` | none | low | all | 0 |

| `N을 해 주세요` | nominalisation, suggest-only | [R] | `N을 해/하여 주…` | `숙제를 해 주세요` | medium | all | 1 news, 1 human essay |
| Object honorific (`3만원 되시겠습니다`) | suggest-only | [S] NIKL: no firm norm | `…되시겠습니다` after a count | `커피 나오셨습니다` is judged variably | n/a | service speech | 0 (regex narrow) |

The last row matters: mixed levels across adjacent sentences is the published AI-draft style (해요체 imperatives next to 합쇼체 statements). Report it to the 말투 track and never auto-fix it.

Deterministic repairs applied by `safe_repair`: spacing (two rules), `-ㄹ께요`, `-시-ㄹ게요` → `-세요`. Only these four are automatic.

Other allowed repairs, which the gate accepts:
- Same-force form swaps, e.g. `하세요` ↔ `해 주세요` ↔ `해 주시기 바랍니다`.
- `하여` → `해`.
- Nominalisation removal.
- Reordering components inside a sentence.
- 겹말 removal (`사전에 미리`).
- Intensifier swaps within one class (`꼭` ↔ `반드시`).
- Splitting a sentence while keeping every action.

## 6. The CTA ledger and gate (`T6/cta_gate.py`)

### What is preserved (per source CTA unit)

Presence, count, action(s), object, force class plus intensifier class, polarity, condition, deadline, channel (digits, URL and handle exact), benefit, and relative placement.

### Weakening and strengthening

- Any change in force level counts, apart from swaps within one level.
- Weakening: `하세요` → `하는 것도 좋습니다` (4→1), `하세요` → `하는 편이 좋습니다` (4→2), `해 주세요` → `연락하시면 안내해 드립니다` (4→3), anything → `할 수 있습니다` (drop).
- Strengthening: `해 보세요` → `반드시 …하십시오` (3→5), adding `꼭`/`지금 바로`, advisory → imperative.
- `~하세요` → `~해 보시는 것도 좋습니다` is a 4→1 drop of three levels (hard).

### Severity (fixed before running any pair set)

- **Hard (block):**
  - `CTA_DROPPED`, `CTA_INVENTED`
  - `CTA_FORCE_WEAKENED`, `CTA_FORCE_STRENGTHENED`
  - `CTA_POLARITY_FLIPPED`
  - `CTA_ACTION_CHANGED`: only for closed-class acts (문의, 상담, 연락, 구독, 공감, 댓글, 이웃추가, 공유, 방문, 다운로드, 클릭, 신청…)
  - `CTA_ACTION_COUNT_CHANGED`
  - `CTA_CONDITION_CHANGED`, `CTA_DEADLINE_CHANGED`
  - `CTA_CHANNEL_CHANGED`, `CTA_BENEFIT_DROPPED`
- **Soft (log and retry):**
  - `CTA_OBJECT_CHANGED`
  - `CTA_ACTION_REPLACED`: open-class verbs such as 제출/준비 can be legitimate substitutions
  - `CTA_MOVED`, `CTA_TO_DEONTIC`, `CTA_FROM_DEONTIC`, `CTA_OFFER_DROPPED`
- **Info:** intensity class swap, same-force form change, speech-level change.

### Alignment

- Equal CTA counts pair in order. Otherwise a banded greedy match on action, lexical overlap and tier.
- An unmatched rewrite unit is not an invention if its actions are a subset of the source's and it is not stronger (a sentence split).
- Deontic bridging only counts deontic statements the rewrite introduced or removed.

### Modes

- `SUPER_SEJONG_CTA=shadow|enforce|off`, mirroring the existing `SUPER_SEJONG_MODE` pattern.
- Start in shadow. Log codes and counts only, no text.
- Idempotent mode (`strict_identity`) for text already processed.

## 7. CTA quality rubric (1–5) with Korean anchors

**Unit:** one source CTA and its rewrite counterpart, judged blind (A/B order randomised).

**Precondition:** the gate must pass. If a ledger violation exists (dropped, weakened, changed), the unit is a fail and is not scored.

### A. 행동의 명확성 (clarity of the action)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | 한 번 읽고 무엇을 할지 안다. 동사가 하나의 구체적 행위. 예) "4월 30일까지 신청서를 제출하세요." |
| 4 | 행동은 분명하나 대상·수단이 문맥에 기댄다. 예) "제출하세요." |
| 3 | 동사가 포괄적이거나 두 갈래로 읽힌다. 예) "관련 사항을 챙겨 보시면 좋겠습니다." |
| 2 | 행동이 명사·상태 표현에 묻혀 추론해야 한다. 예) "신청이 필요한 부분입니다." |
| 1 | 행동이 없거나 무엇을 하라는 건지 알 수 없다. 예) "참고가 되셨으면 합니다." |

### B. 구체성 (specificity: object, channel, deadline, condition, benefit)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | 맥락상 필요한 대상·채널·기한·조건이 모두 있고 숫자·링크가 정확. 예) "궁금한 점은 02-123-4567로 4월 30일까지 전화해 주세요." |
| 4 | 핵심(대상 또는 채널)은 있고 부가 요소 생략이 자연스럽다. 예) "02-123-4567로 전화해 주세요." |
| 3 | 채널이나 대상이 빠져 독자가 되물어야 한다. 예) "문의해 주세요."(어디로?) |
| 2 | 일반어만 있다. 예) "관련 기관에 알아보세요." |
| 1 | 원문의 구체 요소가 바뀌거나 사라졌다(번호·기한·링크 변경). |

### C. 어조 적합성 (tone fit)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 5 | 문서의 말투·높임이 일관되고 요구 강도가 상황에 맞으며 압박적이지 않다. 예) 해요체 글의 "확인해 보세요." |
| 4 | 사소한 어색함 한 곳. 예) 해요체 글에 공문 어투 "하여 주시기 바랍니다"가 끼어듦. |
| 3 | 강도가 과하거나 약해 상황과 어긋난다. 예) "반드시 지금 즉시 문의하십시오"(블로그 안내글), "확인하는 것도 좋겠습니다"(필수 절차 안내). |
| 2 | 높임·어미 오류("하실게요", "되시겠습니다") 또는 한 문장 안의 말투 혼용. |
| 1 | 말투 붕괴·무례, 또는 원문 화자 말투와 충돌(합쇼체 글 속 "확인해라"). |

### Scoring protocol

- Score every source CTA and its rewrite on all three dimensions.
- Report the mean difference (rewrite − source) per dimension and the regression rate (the share of units with a negative difference). The proposed bar is 5% or lower.
- An improvement counts only where the source had a listed defect. Otherwise a higher score is not a win, because the owner wants CTAs unchanged.
- Two raters, weighted kappa 0.6 or higher, calibrated on 20 items first.
- Split results by regime: older posts, recent AI-draft posts, news and essay controls.
- The Hermes drafts' typical closing CTA scores 3 on specificity (no channel in 9 of 9), which tests whether the engine leaves such weaknesses alone.

### Texts with no CTA (must stay CTA-free)

- The rubric does not apply. Run two checks:
  1. Automatic: `CTA_INVENTED` equals 0, and no new directive form of any force appears in the rewrite.
  2. Blind human yes/no: "독자에게 새로 요구하는 행동이 생겼는가?" and "원문에 없던 마무리 호소(구독·공감·문의 권유)가 생겼는가?"
- Required: 0 invented CTAs in 60 or more CTA-free texts, which bounds the invention rate under 5% (rule of three).
- CTA-free items should be at least 30% of the evaluation set. Include tempting ones: service-description closings, how-to explanations, deontic-only paragraphs, news with submission boilerplate.
- Also test that `해야 합니다` does not become an imperative (`CTA_FROM_DEONTIC`, soft).
- Add idempotence: run twice; the second pass must leave every CTA sentence identical, and the share of fully unedited texts is reported.

## 8. What the 윤문 엔진 gets wrong or misses

- **No CTA handling at all.** [M] A grep of the rulebooks and scripts finds none. The only directive tracking is `DEONTIC_RE` in `verify_gates.py` (`~해야 한다`, `필요가 있다`, `요구된다`), which sees 2 of 144 CTA units. Super Sejong v1's `DEONT` events add the `좋습니다` family (36 of 144) and nothing for imperatives or requests.
- **Incidental blocking.** `modality_changed` fires on harmless swaps such as `권장드립니다 → 권합니다`, `필요하시면 → 필요하다면` and `경우가 있습니다 → 될 수 있습니다`. That is 3 of 51 preserving pairs on set A, and 5 of 31 on set B.
- **Rulebook versus published style.** The published AI-draft style still contains the formula CTAs (section 1b). The engine neither removes nor protects them; on the 10 drafts it did nothing.
- **A prompt hint is not enough.** Nothing in the engine verifies that a CTA survives; every miss above passes today.

## 9. Recommendations for v2

1. **Add the CTA ledger and gate** next to the Super Sejong checks in `engine_runtime/workflow.py::verify()`. Port `cta_detect.py` and `cta_gate.py` as they are: standard library, Python 3.9 compatible, about 0.5 s worst case. Use `modality.align` for sentence pairs instead of my matcher. Roll out in shadow mode, enforce the hard set once logs show acceptable false blocks, send the soft set to the conservative retry.
2. **Idempotence by construction.** Run `safe_repair` before and after the model. Put CTA sentences with no wording finding into the prompt as verbatim-protected. Backstop with `strict_identity` for text identified as already processed (a user flag or an engine marker, since provenance cannot be detected).
3. **Only four automatic wording repairs:** two spacing rules, `-ㄹ께요`, `-시-ㄹ게요`. Everything else is suggest-only.
4. **Prompt block** (Korean, one line per CTA): "이 문장은 CTA입니다. 동사·대상·기한·채널·강도(요청/명령/권유 구분)를 유지하고 어색한 표기만 고치세요. 새 CTA를 만들거나 없애지 마세요."
5. **Do not rewrite CTAs for "quality".** The data show recent-post CTAs are weak in channel and specificity, but the owner's rule is "equally strong and equally specific". Raise specificity only through a separate, opt-in suggestion, never silently.
6. **Genre switch.** Turn the CTA gate off for poetry, lyrics and quoted notices. Treat dialogue transcripts and manuals separately; their directives are the content.
7. **Keep `해야 합니다` out of the CTA ledger** but keep the soft bridge codes, so imperative ↔ deontic conversions are logged.
8. **Evidence before any claim of "better CTAs".** Use the rubric with blinded Korean raters. Include 30% or more CTA-free items. Use older posts and non-LLM controls.

## 10. Limits and open questions

- One annotator, no inter-rater agreement. Hand labels cover 1,373 sentences: 408 + 160 + 165 + 640. No native editor reviewed anything.
- The pair sets (221 pairs) and the gate were written by the same author. Sources are constructed, not production text. B and C are no longer held out because I fixed their errors.
- The detector was tuned in several rounds. The only clean numbers are the first run on each fresh set (table in section 4).
- The blog-style numbers are on AI-formula text. Generalisation to the owner's own drafts or other bloggers is untested. Non-blog flagged precision is about 80%, mostly because of policy advice in advisory form.
- The synonym problem (`제출/내다`) is open: action replacement is soft only. A small reviewed lexicon would let part of it become hard. Without it the false-block rate stays low, but a real action swap among open-class verbs is only soft-flagged.
- Wish-form CTAs (`~했으면 좋겠습니다`), `-지 말고` mid-sentence prohibitions and declarative procedural bullets are not detected.
- Dialogue requests remain false positives.
- I could not test the LLM rewrite step itself (idempotence of the model); that needs the runtime and is outside this track.
- Unverified claims are listed in section 2.
