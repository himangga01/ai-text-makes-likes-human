## 0. Bottom line

1. **The valuable material is NIKL's KFL and writing-correction apparatus, not AKS and not any "한글연구소".** NIKL (국립국어원) publishes (a) a learner-error annotation scheme with explicit acceptability (용인가능성) policies, (b) a 25-type list of "errors mainly found in Koreans' own writing", (c) a measured distribution of what its editors correct in official documents, and (d) newly released datasets. Those datasets include a 116,113-post Naver-blog/Instagram corpus, a 1,500-item expert-edit set, and human paraphrases. Details are in §4 and §9.
2. **AKS (한국학중앙연구원) has little for a rewrite engine.** Its 2026 "AI-era" work is person-record data (about 126,000 records) and two open APIs. Its encyclopedia (106k+ articles) is usable as a formal-register human control and nothing else. See §2.
3. **I could not identify any institute named "한글연구소" with AI-era Hangul-education or AI-suitability publications.** What I found instead is in §3, including the popular "AI understands Hangul 95%+" claim, which has no source.
4. **Learner categories are a good vocabulary but a bad prior.** Learners' top layers are 활용 (conjugation), 구어/문어 (register mixing) and 시제 (tense). Native official-document editing is dominated by 띄어쓰기, 문장 부호, 생략 and "문법상 표현". The 윤문 엔진 rulebook contains none of NIKL's human-norm categories (§7).
5. **NIKL's 구어/문어 category is the exact inverse of the owner's principle 1.** For learners, spoken forms in written text are errors; for a blog writer they are voice. I measured NIKL-attested spoken-form items at 1.2 per 10k chars in human essays and **0 in 540k chars of LLM essays**. So the useful engine rule is a **spoken-form survival gate**, not a fixer (§6, R3).
6. **NIKL's own acceptability policy is the best false-positive guard available.** Examples: honorific agreement is judged only within a sentence; 현재/미래 관형사형 both accepted; short negation accepted except for certain predicates; word order edits use minimal-edit. These should be encoded as "leave alone" rules (R2).
7. **LLMs are weak exactly where norms depend on sound-linked orthography.** KoGEM reports LLMs struggle with phonological rules and pronunciation. A 2026 comparison found ChatGPT 5.2 at 71.1% and Gemini 3 at 92.2% on 90 items, with "grammatical hallucinations" and confident wrong explanations. Closed-class orthography is therefore a good candidate for deterministic gates (R5).
8. **Philology contributes almost nothing new beyond report G.** The 해례본 final-consonant statement supports "never infer spelling from sound". Translationese sources (Japanese, English, Classical Chinese) are real, but the corpus study I opened (Choi 2016) says translationese features occur in non-translated Korean too. That matches the EVALUATION decision to keep those rules suggest-only.
9. **"Hangul is AI-optimal" is not evidence.** Measured token cost per Hangul syllable is 1.33 (cl100k) vs 0.75 (o200k), so tokenizer choice dominates (§6).
10. **Caveat on how I worked.** The session's WebSearch budget (200) ran out partway. After that I used direct page fetches, OpenAlex/Crossref APIs, and site pages. Items I could not open are marked.

## 4. KFL education: learner-error taxonomies mapped to a native-writer engine

### 4.1 NIKL learner-corpus annotation scheme [PDF-read]

Source: NIKL, *2020년 한국어 학습자 말뭉치 연구 및 구축* (연구책임자 한송화), 413 pp. Page: https://www.korean.go.kr/front/reportData/reportDataView.do?mn_id=&report_seq=1056&pageIndex=1. Licence on the page: 제4유형 (attribution, non-commercial, no derivatives). I quote only facts and short labels. The scheme is in Appendix 2 (report pp. 147–228).

Three annotation dimensions:
- **오류 위치** (the morpheme or POS where the error sits).
- **오류 양상** (mode): 누락 (OM), 첨가 (ADD), 대치 (REP), 오형태 (MIF). A footnote says simple spelling or conjugation errors are classed as 오형태 because the first three make no sense for them.
- **오류 층위** (layer). There are four groups:
  - 발음: 음소, 음절, 음운규칙, plus two provisional codes. Spoken data only.
  - 형태: 합성법, 파생법, 곡용, 활용, 품사.
  - 통사: 높임 (SH), 시제 (ST), 사동 (SC), 피동 (SP), 부정 (SN), 어순 (WO).
  - 담화: 지시 (DR), 접속 (DC), 담화표지 (DM), 구어/문어 (DS).

Annotation principles stated in the guideline (pp. 148–149):
- An error is judged against **grammaticality**; "용인가능성" is applied strictly and consistently.
- Judgement is at **sentence level**. Context is used only for DR and DC.
- When a case is both lexical and grammatical, **the function word wins** (grammar first).

### 4.4 Learner category → native-writer engine action → overlap with LLM-generated awkwardness

"Action" is my reasoning; "overlap" is partly measured and partly reasoning.

| Learner/NIKL category | Native-writer relevance | LLM-generated overlap | Proposed v2 action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 굴절(활용), 곡용, 오형태 | Rare for adult native writers; dominant for learners (21.8%) | Only as **edit artefacts** (a word is swapped and the ending or particle form is left stale) | Mechanical check on **rewrite-introduced** mismatches only. Existing josa gate covers 10 families. Possible additions: vocative 아/야, 이든/든, 이라도/라도, 이라서/라서, 이라면/라면. Low priority |
| 시제 | Real for narrative blogs, but NIKL excludes modal 겠, and accepts both -는 and -ㄹ before nouns | LLMs shift tense when merging or splitting sentences | **Guard**: tense preserved per sentence. Do not "fix" |
| 높임 | Learner 8.6%; official docs 0.29% | Introduced when endings or honorific elements are altered | **Guard**: judged within a sentence only. Flag only an introduced mismatch (e.g., 께서 or -시- added in one place and not another) |
| 어순 | Learner 5.6%; docs 0.98%; NIKL says tolerance is high and edits must be minimal | Rare; LLMs may reorder clauses | Ignore by default. Fix only misplaced 성분부사 (NIKL's own criterion) |
| 구어/문어 (DS) | 17.3% of learner layers, but for blog genre it is deliberate voice | **Inverse relation**: LLM output contains almost none (§6) | Never fix. Use as a **register-drift signal** (R3) |
| 사동/피동 | Learner 2.0% / 3.6%. NIKL counts only -이/히/리/기/우/구/추- passives and -어지다, not 되다/당하다/-게 되다; double passive is an 'addition' error | Double passive is the vendor rule A-8; measured very rare (§6) | Mechanical rule possible but low yield; do not expand to all passives |
| 부정 | Learner 2.2%; NIKL accepts short negation except for compound/derived words and long predicates | Rare | Guard only (meaning guard already covers negation) |
| 접속, 지시 | Learner 3.0% / 1.5%; native paragraph writing also shows connection problems (Shin 2015) | High: connector runs, demonstrative+through frames (EVALUATION §3) | T5 (flow) |
| 호응 (주-술, 목-술, 부-술, 수식어-피수식어) | Core native-writer awkwardness (4 of the 25 types) | Introduced when clauses are merged or nominalised | **Primary fix target** and a rewrite-introduced check; needs a parser, so offline or hint-level |
| 조사 (choice) | 45% of learner top-100 errors; docs 4.5% | **Edit artefact**: predicate swapped, case frame not updated (the NIKL example 조력 존엄사에 비관적으로 바라보는 → 를) | Check particle vs. new predicate's case frame when a predicate lemma changes; hint-level without a valency lexicon |
| 띄어쓰기, 맞춤법, 문장 부호 | 24% of official-doc corrections (spacing + punctuation) | LLMs usually fix these silently | **Decision for owner**: spacing edits are orthography, not "awkward Korean"; count them in a separate change budget |
| 외래어/외국어/로마자 | 10.3% of docs ("외국어·외래어 오남용") | Possible in tech blogs | Out of scope unless the source has an explicit finding |
| 생략 (omitted components) | 15.5% of docs | LLMs may over-restore omitted subjects (changes voice and meaning) | **Guard**: do not restore dropped subjects/particles that the source omitted; NIKL treats spoken particle omission as acceptable |
| 번역투 | Not in NIKL's list | High, but rules fire on human prose too (EVALUATION §3) | Suggest-only (see §5) |

### 4.5 Acceptability (용인가능성) rules NIKL used, directly usable as false-positive guards [PDF-read, report pp. 207–223]

- **Honorifics:** because speakers do not apply 높임 consistently, NIKL "uses agreement within one sentence as the necessary criterion". If any honorific element is realised in a sentence, the unrealised one is the error. 나/저 is resolved by the sentence ending.
- **Mixed endings in learners (-다 and -ㅂ니다):** NIKL corrects to the **more frequent ending** (written) and treats spoken differently. This is a learner rule. For a native blog writer, majority-ending normalisation would erase voice. Do not port it.
- **Tense:** 관형사형 현재 (-는) vs 미래 (-ㄹ) both possible ⇒ not an error. -겠- with modal meaning is not annotated as tense.
- **Negation:** short negation is acceptable except for compound/derived words and long predicates.
- **Word order:** because order is free, errors need a minimal-edit standard; only 성분부사 position is judged.
- **Spoken text:** particle omission is accepted; 준말 are accepted if the 표준국어대사전 lists them (e.g., 맘).
- **Register arbiter:** 표준국어대사전's "문어적/구어적 표현" labels decide written vs spoken. Unlisted items are discussed and then catalogued.

### 4.6 Speech levels in KFL research

- Choi Juhee 2013 (45 letters by advanced learners, paragraph level): transitions between 합쇼체↔해요체, 합쇼체↔해체, 합쇼체↔해라체, 해요체↔해체, 해요체↔해라체 and 해라체↔해체 occurred. **합쇼체→해라체 and 해요체→해라체 were judged "not allowed".** [abstract-only] https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART001783666
- Kim and Yang 2020 (10 intermediate learners): there is a gap between how learners perceive and how they use 합쇼체/해요체 switching in presentations. [abstract-only] https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002581394
- Ahn Ju-ho 2017 proposes teaching a linear 하십시오체–해요체–해체–해라체 system and dropping 하오체/하게체 from the core. [abstract-only] https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE07231807
- Implication (T2 owns the final design): the allowed in-text transitions for a rewrite are those already present in the source. A rewrite must not introduce a new level or a forbidden transition (esp. toward 해라체 from 합쇼체/해요체). NIKL's majority-ending normalisation is a learner rule and must not be used.

### 4.7 Other taxonomies

- **KAGAS (Yoon et al., ACL 2023)** [PDF-read]: https://aclanthology.org/2023.acl-long.371/. It defines 14 Korean edit types (INS, DEL, WS, WO, SPELL, PUNCT, SHORT, VERB, ADJ, NOUN, PART, END, MOD, CONJ) by morpheme-level alignment.
  - Three datasets: Kor-Learner (28,426 sentence pairs), Kor-Native (17,559) and Kor-Lang8 (109,559).
  - Kor-Native is built from dictation of TTS audio by the general public; **word spacing is the dominant native error (about 50% of Kor-Native edits)**.
  - Human check of KAGAS labels (26 sentences per type, 3 raters): INS/DEL/WS 100%; WO/SPELL/PART 97.44%; PUNCT 98.72%; END 87.18%; MOD 89.74%; VERB 79.49%; NOUN 75.64%; ADJ/SHORT 73.08%; CONJ 43.59%. Cohen's kappa is moderate (average 0.4656).
  - The paper discards VERB:SVA and adds WS compared with ERRANT.
  - Licence: the repo uses a modified MIT licence; Kor-Learner and Kor-Native are **non-commercial only** [summariser, https://github.com/soyoung97/Standard_Korean_GEC].
- **KoLLA v2.0** (Song, Lim, Park 2025): 100 essays, 1,419 sentences, 2,838 references (2 per sentence); extended MRU-style labels (M/R/U with WB, SPELL, ORDER, postposition/ending); GPL-3.0; Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.15287129 [summariser, https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.00261 and https://arxiv.org/html/2505.00261].
- **NIKL L2 corpus re-annotation** (Park et al., arXiv 2605.30545, 2026-05-28): argues word-level labels collapse Korean-specific distinctions; reports 3,613 files of NIKL data; reconstruction accuracy improved from 95.21% to 98.77% [summariser, https://arxiv.org/html/2605.30545].
- **Replacement errors** (Jeon 2021, 문화와 융합 43(10)): nouns show mostly semantic substitutions, verbs/adjectives mostly grammatical ones [abstract-only]: https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002769268
- **Annotation practice** (Kim Hansaem 2017, 배달말 61): NIKL's learner morphological tagging follows the **21세기 세종계획** guidelines for compatibility with native corpora [abstract-only]: https://kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART002303341
- **Annotation guidance** (Yoo and Kang 2019, 언어사실과 관점 48) describes the three-part annotation (position, mode, layer) and the tension between efficiency and consistency [abstract-only]: https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE09405827

### 4.8 What this says about LLM norm competence

- **KoGEM** (Kim et al., 2025, arXiv 2506.01237; CC BY 4.0 + KOGL Type 1): 1,500 (README: 1,524) multiple-choice items, 5 categories and 16 subcategories, 27 LLMs evaluated. LLMs struggle with phonological rules and pronunciation [summariser, https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01237, https://github.com/SungHo3268/KoGEM].
- **Spell-checker comparison** (Kim Young-il 2026, 교양교육연구 20(1): 211): 90 items across orthography, vocabulary and sentence level. Naver 80%, Daum 86.7%, Bareun 83.3%, Gemini 3 92.2%, ChatGPT 5.2 71.1%. The author describes "grammatical hallucinations and post-hoc rationalizations" [summariser, https://j-kagedu.or.kr/journal/view.php?doi=10.46392/kjge.2026.20.1.211]. The sample is small.
- **Rater reliability** (NIKL 2024 report 1203, PDF p. 26): for the 2023 argumentative-writing rubric re-score, two-rater agreement on the 표현 total was exact 0.24, similar 0.49, correlation 0.36. The report concludes the error-count-based 표현 criterion caused inconsistency [PDF-read, https://www.korean.go.kr/front/reportData/reportDataView.do?mn_id=207&report_seq=1203&pageIndex=1]. Implication: expert disagreement on "naturalness" is high, so T7 should expect it and avoid a single error-count score.

## 5. Philology and Hangul structure

- **Final consonants.** The 훈민정음 해례 종성해 states that eight finals (ㄱ ㆁ ㄷ ㄴ ㅂ ㅁ ㅅ ㄹ) suffice, with ㅅ usable for other sibilant finals (example 빗곶, 영의갗) [Wikisource transcription; the fetch rendered one example garbled, so I describe it in words]: https://ko.wikisource.org/wiki/%ED%9B%88%EB%AF%BC%EC%A0%95%EC%9D%8C. The 합자해 states that initial, medial and final sounds combine to form a character [same source].
  - Implication: 1446 spelling was phonemic at the coda, while modern orthography is morphophonemic (report G, NIKL answers). **Never derive spelling from sound.** G's invariants already state this. Nothing new for the josa module: it keys on the presence or absence of a written coda and on ㄹ for (으)로, from Unicode arithmetic (EVALUATION §4).
- **Code history.** The 1990 NIKL 새국어생활 article "표준 한글 코드는 조합형이어야 한다" (강태진) records that the KS C 5601 완성형 code could represent only **2,350 of 11,172** modern syllables [summariser, https://www.korean.go.kr/nkview/nklife/1990_2/21_8.html]. Implication: old documents and CP949 round-trips can leave missing or substituted syllables; keep the input-integrity checks from G (NFC view plus offset map).
- **Translationese sources.**
  - Japanese: Oh 2014, ~적 (的) translation; literal one-to-one translation misleads [abstract-only]: https://www.earticle.net/Article/A317595
  - English: Lee 2008, translationese types in English→Korean science translation [abstract-only]: https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE01596862
  - Classical Chinese: Nam 2011, literal rendering and readability in 한문 translation [abstract-only]: https://www.dbpia.co.kr/Article/NODE01643850 ; Seo 2008 on 非文 in government-funded classical translation [abstract-only]: https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/ci/sereArticleSearch/ciSereArtiView.kci?sereArticleSearchBean.artiId=ART001305045
  - Early modern: Kim Dong-eon 1996, 개화기 translation style [abstract-only, generic]: https://www.dbpia.co.kr/Journal/ArticleDetail/NODE06568238
  - **Choi Hee-kyung 2016** (번역학연구 17(1): 147–169): in comparable corpora, "typical translationese features appear in both translated and non-translated texts" for English from/through; she proposes viewing it as language contact [abstract-only, https://www.dbpia.co.kr/journal/articleDetail?nodeId=NODE06647253]. This is the best academic support for the EVALUATION rejection of 에 의해, 에 대해, 을 가지다 and similar rules (they fire on human prose).
  - Implication: keep translationese rules suggest-only. Fix only when it creates a 호응 break or blocks comprehension.
- **"세종" naming.** The temple name is 世宗; the posthumous name is 莊憲英文睿武仁聖明孝大王. Hangul was created in 1443 and promulgated in 1446 (secondary source) [summariser, https://ko.wikipedia.org/wiki/%EC%84%B8%EC%A2%85_%28%EC%A1%B0%EC%84%A0%29]. I did not verify against the Sillok, and the summariser's account of a petition to change the name was inconsistent, so I discard it. **No engineering implication.**
  - The name does collide with the **21세기 세종계획** (the national corpus project and the Sejong POS tagset). Kiwi's tagset is "based on the Sejong standard" and Kiwi trained on 21세기 세종계획 plus 모두의 말뭉치 corpora; it is Apache-2.0 for 0.24.0 [summariser, https://github.com/bab2min/Kiwi]. Docs should say "Super Sejong Engine" in full to avoid confusion with "Sejong tagset" or "Sejong corpus".
- Report G already covers Unicode syllable arithmetic, jamo, NFC/NFKC. I add nothing there.

## 7. What the 윤문 엔진 gets wrong or misses in this area

- **Its rulebook is a "tell" taxonomy, not a norm taxonomy.** In `vendor/humanize-engine/skills/humanize-korean/references/` (about 1,478 lines across `ai-tell-taxonomy.md`, `quick-rules.md`, `diagnosis-rules.md`, `rewriting-playbook.md`), mention counts by grep: 호응 1, 높임 0, 주술 0, 맞춤법 0, 어순 2, 띄어쓰기 2, 문어 1. Its sections are A 번역투, B English quotation, C structural patterns, D signature phrases, E rhythm, F modification/redundancy, G hedging, H connectors, I formal nouns, J decoration. None of the NIKL 25 types (e.g., the four 호응 types, 조사 선택, 높임 agreement, 시제) is a diagnosis category. This is a mention count, not a rule-by-rule audit.
- **It targets LLM tells** (EVALUATION §3 rejected several because they fire on human prose) **and treats them like errors.** NIKL's vocabulary separates "violations of norm" from style.
- **No acceptability policy.** There is no tolerance list like §4.5, so natural text gets edited.
- **Register treatment is one-directional** (sanitise AI style). It has no notion that spoken forms are legitimate and must survive.
- **Learner priors would be wrong for the engine, and so would LLM-tell priors for the same reason:** learners and LLMs err in different places than native bloggers.

## 8. Recommendations for v2

Each gives trigger, counterexample, false-positive risk and genre boundary where it is a rule. All are proposals, not measured gates unless stated.

**R1. Make NIKL's 25 types plus the 9 knowledge categories the human-norm layer of the diagnosis vocabulary.** Tag every rule as `human-norm(<id>)`, `LLM-tell`, or `voice-guard`. Counterexample: translationese rules stay `LLM-tell` and suggest-only. Genre: the list is built from university and adult argumentative writing and official documents; blog genre needs checking (R8).

**R2. Encode the NIKL acceptability policy (§4.5) as "leave alone" defaults.** Trigger for any edit: if the case is in the tolerated set, only an introduced mismatch counts. FP risk: low, because the policy comes from an expert annotation project. Boundary: NIKL applies sentence-level judgement; flow and CTA tracks may override at text level.

**R3. Add a spoken-form survival gate (register preservation).**
- Trigger: the source contains tokens from a colloquial list (the NIKL items, plus 표준국어대사전 구어 labels); the rewrite removes more than k of them without a finding.
- Counterexample: a source typo of a colloquial form; quoted speech.
- FP risk: unknown; measured rate in human essays is 1.2 per 10k chars, so most documents trigger nothing.
- Genre: blog and conversational text. It requires the blog baseline (R8) to set k; my data cannot.
- Cross-check with T2 before use.

**R4. Speech-level transitions.** A rewrite may use only transitions already present in the source and must not introduce 합쇼체/해요체→해라체 (Choi 2013). Do not apply NIKL's majority-ending correction. T2 owns the final form.

**R5. Closed-class orthography table for sound-linked norms.**
- Trigger: a token matches a finite list drawn from the norm text itself, e.g., 한글 맞춤법 제29항 (ㄹ→ㄷ): 반짇고리, 사흗날, 삼짇날, 섣달, 숟가락, 이튿날, 잗주름, 푿소, 섣부르다, 잗다듬다, 잗다랗다 (listed in the NIKL 국어 지식 기초 자료 2024 example [PDF-read]).
- Counterexample: dialect, quotation, brand names.
- FP risk: very low for a closed list.
- Rationale: KoGEM and Kim 2026 show LLMs are weakest here. Build the table from the public norm (kornorms), not from the corpus, to avoid redistribution limits.
- Genre: all but dialogue and intentional misspelling.

**R6. Edit-type audit using coarse KAGAS-like classes.** Report, per rewrite, counts of edits grouped as orthographic (SPELL/WS/PUNCT), grammatical (PART/END/CONJ), lexical (NOUN/VERB/ADJ/MOD) and structural (INS/DEL/WO). Use only the reliable coarse groups (KAGAS fine labels range from 43.6% to 100% correct). Flag a rewrite whose lexical plus structural edits are high while the source has no human-norm finding. The threshold must be calibrated on NIKL expert edits (R8), not guessed.

**R7. Keep the josa gate's scope, and extend it modestly.** Learner data show particle **choice** (replacement and omission, 86.74% of particle errors) is the real problem; allomorph mismatch is mostly an edit artefact. Add the families listed in §4.4 only if shadow-mode logs show them. Add a particle-vs-predicate case-frame check only after a predicate swap, and only at hint level without a valency lexicon.

**R8. Data requests to NIKL (모두의 말뭉치; application reason "인공지능(모델/서비스/제품 등) 개발 및 개선" or "성능 평가" is offered on the form [HTML-read]).** Priority:
1. 온라인 게시 자료 말뭉치 2025 (Naver blog 73,176 + Instagram 42,937 posts, 2023–2024, recruited posters). Use for blog false-positive rates, spoken-form rates and speech-level priors. This is the single most valuable resource for the owner's genre.
2. 글쓰기 첨삭 인스트럭션 말뭉치 2024 (expert minimal edits). Use for rule precision and edit-size distribution; remember the sentence-level always-an-error selection.
3. 유사 문장 말뭉치 (human paraphrases) to measure meaning-guard overblock on real paraphrases (§9).
4. NIKLex (§9) to extend antonym and direction axes.
5. 국어 지식 기초 자료 2024 for rule regression tests.
Note: application period is at least 5 days; institutions need an official letter; the maximum use period is bounded by the source licence period of each corpus.

**R9. Licence bookkeeping.** Record, per NIKL-derived rule or lexicon entry, the licence type of the page it came from. Recent NIKL reports carry "제1유형 + AI유형" (AI-training use allowed commercially, derivatives allowed, no attribution needed for training; conditions: outputs must not be identical or substantially similar to the public work, direct quotations need source attribution, and resale of training datasets built from the work is prohibited). I read this on report pages 339, 1184, 1203, 1206 and 1207. Older reports differ: the 2020 learner-corpus report is 제4유형 (non-commercial, no derivatives). Per-corpus licences in 모두의 말뭉치 are set in each application agreement.

**R10. Do not use learner priors as native priors.** Learner top layers (활용, 구어/문어, 시제) differ from native official-document corrections (§4.3). Use the 4.3(c) table as the only published native base-rate, and replace it with blog baselines (R8).

**R11. Evaluation-side.** Expect low inter-rater agreement on 표현 naturalness (0.24 exact, 0.36 correlation in NIKL's own re-score). Use paired preference with multiple raters rather than error counts; this supports T7's design.

**R12. Tokens, not characters, for backend budgets** (§3, §6).

**R13. Naming.** Always write "Super Sejong Engine" in full in logs and docs to avoid confusion with the Sejong tagset or corpus.
