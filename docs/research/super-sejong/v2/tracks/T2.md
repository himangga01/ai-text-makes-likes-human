### 1. What 말투 means here (definitions with sources)

**Speech level (상대높임법)** is read from the final predicate's ending.

- [C] 표준국어대사전 definitions:
  - 하십시오체: "상대편을 아주 높이는 종결형" ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%ED%95%98%EC%8B%AD%EC%8B%9C%EC%98%A4%EC%B2%B4)).
  - 해요체: 보통 높임, 비격식 ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%ED%95%B4%EC%9A%94%EC%B2%B4)).
  - 해라체: "아주 낮추는 종결형" ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%ED%95%B4%EB%9D%BC%EC%B2%B4)).
  - 해체: 해라·하게 자리에 두루 쓰는 비격식체 ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%ED%95%B4%EC%B2%B4)).
  - 하오체: "현대 국어의 구어에서는 거의 쓰지 않는다" ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%ED%95%98%EC%98%A4%EC%B2%B4)).
  - 하게체 ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%ED%95%98%EA%B2%8C%EC%B2%B4)).
- [C] NIKL 온라인가나다:
  - 격식체 = 해라·하게·하오·하십시오; 비격식체 = 해·해요 ([Q&A 314653](https://korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&pageIndex=1&qna_seq=314653&searchCondition=&searchKeyword=)).
  - '-세요' is a 해요 ending and '-ㅂ니다' a 하십시오 ending ([Q&A 310783](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&qna_seq=310783)).
  - 해요체 is 비격식 ([Q&A 319561](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&qna_seq=319561&pageIndex=1&searchCondition=&searchKeyword=)).
  - Indirect honorification ("커피 나오셨습니다") is not prohibited by the norms, and judgments vary ([Q&A 332098](https://www.korean.go.kr/front/onlineQna/onlineQnaView.do?mn_id=216&qna_seq=332098&pageIndex=1)).
  - 하오체 청유 is "-ㅂ시다"; 하십시오체 청유 is "-시지요" or "-십시다" ([NIKL on X](https://x.com/urimal365/status/578063939797299201)).
- [C] '-답니다' is "하십시오할 자리… 친근하게 가르쳐 주거나 자랑하는" ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%EB%8B%B5%EB%8B%88%EB%8B%A4)). It is therefore 하십시오체 that carries a separate "teaching" stance.
- [C] '-네' is listed first as 하게할 자리 ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%EB%84%A4)); '-는가' likewise ([stdict](https://stdict.korean.go.kr/search/searchResult.do?pageSize=10&searchKeyword=%EB%8A%94%EA%B0%80)). Only the first sense was visible on the search pages, so the full entries are unverified.
- [C] Real usage alternates levels within one discourse:
  - 임지룡, 「학교문법 상대 높임법의 새로운 이해」, 韓民族語文學 69 ([pdf](http://emunhak.com/chart/69_12_limjr.pdf)). It cites Sohn (1983) and 왕문용 (2008): the same speaker alternates 하십시오/해요 and 해라/해 in one discourse without a difference in level. It also reports drama-script shares (해체 41%, 해라체 15%, 해요체 41%, 하십시오체 17%) and says 하오/하게 are barely used by students.
  - 박지순, 「한국어 모어 화자의 상대높임법 인식 양상」, 韓民族語文學 73 ([pdf](http://emunhak.com/chart/73_05_parkjs.pdf)): 하십시오체 and 해요체 differ in the system, "사용 양상에서는 큰 차이가 없었다".
  - [R] This is why mixing is natural and must not be "fixed". Per owner decision 1, the gate still keeps each sentence's own level.

**Operational label set** (implemented in `T2/voice.py`):
- Levels: HAPSYO, HAEYO, HAERA (including 하라체 and written self-question '-ㄴ가?'), HAE (including '-네', '-군', '-지', '-ㄹ까'), HAO (including '-ㅂ시다', per NIKL), HAGE (including '-구먼'; '-네' and '-ㄴ가' only with 자네 or 안녕하신가 context), NOMINAL (명사형/개조식 '-음', '-기', bare noun) and NONE (connective, comma continuation, vocative, no predicate).
- Mood: DECL, INTERR, IMPER, PROPOS, EXCL.
- -시- on the final predicate complex (the last eojeol, or the last two when the last is 것/거/건/겁…).
- Stance ending families: evidential (더라고요), reason (거든요), shared (잖아요), discovery (네요, 군요), teaching (답니다), confirm (죠), background (는데요), promise (ㄹ게요), hearsay (대요, 래요).
- Persona: 저/제/저희, 나/내, 우리, 필자, and self-introduction "OOO 행정사입니다".
- Addressee: 여러분, OO님, 너, 자네.
- Humble forms: 드리다, 여쭙다, 뵙다, 모시다.
- CMC and colloquial tokens (owner decision 2): ㅋㅋ, ㅎㅎ, ㅠㅠ, ^^, ~, emoji; spellings 할께, 넹, 걍, 젤, 넘, 습니당, 어여; discourse markers 사실, 뭐, 진짜, 완전, 아, 음 … (sentence-initial, except the intensifiers).
- Quoted speech keeps its own level and is checked separately.

### 3. How LLM writing drifts 말투 (KatFish)

Measured with the Kiwi backend (`katfish_drift.py`, `katfish_doclevel.py`; results in `results/katfish_*`). Unit: body sentences, then documents. **Caveat:** these are generations from a prompt, not rewrites.

**Essays** (sentences: human 1,984; GPT-4o 3,625; Qwen2 2,981; Solar 1,998; Llama3.1 1,286)

| Feature | Human | LLMs |
|---|---|---|
| HAPSYO share of sentences | 44.5% | 81.5–97.2% |
| HAERA share of sentences | 51.1% | 2.3–16.4% |
| Documents with HAERA dominant | 91/181 = 0.50 [0.43, 0.57] | 0.02–0.17 |
| Discourse markers per 100 sentences | 3.73 | 0.15–0.44 |
| Documents with ≥1 discourse marker | 0.24 | 0.01–0.07 |
| Plain first person 나 per 100 sentences | 7.0 | 0.15–1.16 |
| Documents with ≥1 나 | 0.38 | 0.01–0.18 |
| Generic 우리 per 100 sentences | 5.7 | 9–27 |
| Documents with ≥3 우리 | 0.07 | 0.19–0.66 |
| Propositive per 100 sentences | 1.06 | ≤0.10 |
| Documents with dominant level <90% (mixed) | 32/180 | GPT 1/181; Solar 1/140; Qwen 12/181; Llama 11/88 |
| Emoticons | rare | ≈0 |

- The essay generation prompts were themselves 합쇼체 (122) or 해요체 (59).
- [R] LLMs adopt the instruction's speech level instead of the genre norm. Humans mix levels; LLMs are uniform.
- Interrogatives are **not** lower in LLM text at document level: human 0.15, GPT 0.20, Qwen 0.22 (LLMs use rhetorical "~할까요?").

**Abstracts:** human 99.8% HAERA. Solar is 87% HAPSYO and Qwen 53%, a genre-level drift.

**Drift patterns most likely in a rewrite** [R; prioritised by these measurements and the vendor rules]:
1. 해요→합니다 normalisation, especially CTAs ("확인하세요" → "확인하시기 바랍니다").
2. Uniform level imposed on a deliberately mixed paragraph.
3. Stance endings (더라고요, 거든요, 잖아요, 네요, 죠, 답니다) flattened to plain -어요 or -습니다.
4. Questions and propositives turned into statements.
5. -시- added or dropped.
6. 저 becomes 나 or 우리; persona dropped; humble 드리다 dropped.
7. Discourse markers, CMC tokens and colloquial spellings removed.
8. Upward shifts such as 했→하였, and 개조식 lines turned into full sentences (and back).
9. A split or merge that changes one half's level.

**What the 윤문 엔진 gets wrong** [M and file evidence]:
- Its quick-rules E-7 says "격식 등급 하나로 일관 유지" and its E-2 says "종결어미 다양화… 명사형 종결을 섞음" (`vendor/.../quick-rules.md` lines 76–77; `ai-tell-taxonomy.md` §E-2, §E-7). Both tell the writer to cause drifts 2 and 8.
- Its only register check (`checks.check_register`: ≥3 '~요' endings in the source and fewer than half kept) caught 1/64 of my drift pairs and 0/104 post-level conversions on the owner's posts. T1 measured 0/17 at the 50% level.
- res4 measured that the same check catches 4/25 register flips in its own pairs.

**Literature that transfers:**
- [C] Hwang, Kim & Jung (2021), "Context-Aware NMT for Korean Honorific Expressions", Electronics 10(13):1589 ([pdf](https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/13a8/ade0e6c73ef11669a7b428569f19fd1686c6.pdf)): labels honorific style with a rule heuristic on sentence-final endings and uses context-aware post-editing to fix inconsistent sentence-level honorifics. This supports an ending-rule classifier plus a post-edit repair step.
- [C] StyleKQC (Cho et al., LREC 2022, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.13439)): a formal/informal paraphrase corpus of questions and commands.
- [C] SmileStyle ([GitHub](https://github.com/smilegate-ai/korean_smile_style_dataset)): 17 parallel styles, 27,761 sentences, CC-BY-NC 4.0, "toy dataset" with errors. I used it for evaluation only; it must not ship.
- [C] GYAFC (Rao & Tetreault, NAACL 2018, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.06535)): evaluates on style, content and fluency together, and warns about automatic metrics. That is the evaluation frame for section 6.
- [C] Park, Do & Lee (2025, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.20811)): LLMs over-correct in grammatical error correction. This motivates "no edit is valid".
- [C] Lee (2026, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07447)): an LLM-associated vocabulary shift in Korean abstracts; it covers vocabulary, not endings.
- I found no published Korean work that measures speech-level preservation in LLM rewriting.
- **Not verified (403 or not opened):** Jung & Kim (2025, ScienceDirect) on LLM subject honorification; Brown's papers on -yo/-supnita shifting.

### 4. Voice-lock gate specification

Code: `T2/gate.py` and `T2/repair.py`. Evaluation: `eval_gate.py`, `eval_repair.py`, `eval_cmc.py`, `stress.py`, `repair_scale.py`, `vendor_cmp.py`.

**Alignment and segments**
- Sentences are aligned monotonically with 1:1, 1:2, 2:1, 1:0 and 0:1 moves, using character-bigram Dice scores.
- Headings and list lines are compared as their own units; a 개조식 line must stay 개조식.

**Per-sentence hard violations**
- `level`: any change of the fine level, including NOMINAL↔finite.
- `mood` (DECL↔EXCL is soft).
- `hon_si_dropped` / `hon_si_added` on the final predicate.
- `quote_level`: speech level inside a quotation changed.
- `split_level`: one piece of a split takes a different level.
- `merge_across_levels`: two source sentences of different levels merged.
- `merge_lost_mood` / `merge_lost_hon_si`.
- `inserted_level`: a new sentence with a different level from its neighbour.

**Soft violations**
- `stance`: ending family changed.
- `persona_swap`, `persona_dropped`, `persona_added`, `humble_dropped`.
- `emoticon_dropped` / `emoticon_added`.
- `colloquial_spelling_changed`, `discourse_marker_dropped`, `colloquial_added`.
- `hon_si_elsewhere`: -시- count changed anywhere in the sentence.
- `written_upshift` (했→하였).

**Tolerated**
- Any rewording that keeps the level, mood, -시-, stance family and markers: ~필요합니다→~해야 합니다; 인정이 됩니다→인정됩니다; 했는데→하니; 하였다→했다 (down-shift); 을까요 variants.
- With the opt-in option `allow_cmc_normalise=True`, the CMC, spelling and discourse codes become info only.

**Document profile:** used only for the prompt, never for a decision.

**Repair, in order**
1. **Graft:** copy the writer's own final eojeol back onto the rewritten clause, plus the final punctuation. Allowed only when the lemma keys match (ending syllables stripped, contractions such as 해/합/했→하 normalised). Graft is not used for a mood change, a quote, or a NOMINAL/NONE sentence.
2. **Restore** trailing emoticons.
3. **Revert** the sentence when anything is still violated.
4. **Revert the group** for a split or merge that is still violated.

[R] Grafting never invents a conjugation, because it only reuses the writer's own form.

**Measured results**

*Pair set (preserving 64 / drifting 64):*
- Hard: false positives 0/64; caught 49/64; hard-expected drifts caught as hard 48/49. The miss is d42: -시- added inside a modifier. It is soft-flagged after the `hon_si_elsewhere` check, which was added after the blind run.
- Hard plus soft, first blind run (`results/gate_run1_blind.keep`): 0/64 false positives, 63/64 caught.
- Current: 3/64 flagged, 64/64 caught. The 3 are:
  - p26 and p41, 진짜→정말: drift under owner decision 2, written before it.
  - p19, 궁금하신→궁금한: a real -시- loss my label had tolerated.
- Kiwi backend: identical.

*Supplement for owner decision 2 (written before the CMC check existed):*
- 8 preserving / 12 drifting pairs.
- Before the check: 0/8 flagged, 5/12 caught. After: 0/8 flagged, 12/12 caught.
- With opt-in normalisation: 2/12 still flagged; both are real level or stance changes.

*Repair on the pair set:* 26 grafts, 33 reverts, 4 emoticon restores, 3 group reverts. No residual violations. 3/64 preserving pairs altered, which are the 3 reclassified pairs. On the CMC set: 0 residual, 0/8 preserving pairs altered.

*Scale:*
- 0 false flags on voice-neutral edits of 1,202 paragraphs (5,884 sentences).
- 620/620 single-sentence drifts caught and restored exactly.
- First scale run before fixes: 4 false flags; persona '내 ' and '-신' in nouns such as 출신 and 신청 were fixed.
- Earlier CMC runs flagged nouns: 사실 관계, and 전용 read as the cute coda '용'. The fixes: discourse markers count only sentence-initially (intensifiers anywhere), and cute codas are matched only on predicate patterns.

- [R] Real LLM rewrites will be harder than both: re-chunking, partial clause moves, 하다-verb swaps.
- Run in shadow mode on production diagnostics first.
- Soft codes should first be used for retry and repair, not hard rejection.

### 5. Prompt instructions (proposal, not measured)

Give the writer a **per-sentence ending sheet**: each source sentence ID with its level, mood, -시- flag, final eojeol, and any CMC, spelling or discourse tokens. Example: `S7 해요체·명령·시 「확인하세요」 keep:[~]`.

Add the document profile only as context, for example: "합쇼 82%, 해요 CTA·질문 섞임 — 섞임은 의도된 것".

Instructions:
- "각 문장의 마지막 어절(종결형)과 문장 유형, '-시-', 저/저희·드리다, ㅋㅋ·^^·~·할께 같은 표기, 사실·뭐·진짜 같은 담화 표지는 원문 그대로 둔다. 문장 안의 어색한 부분만 고친다. 문장을 나누거나 합칠 때 두 문장의 문체가 다르면 나누거나 합치지 않는다. 질문·권유·명령을 평서문으로 바꾸지 않는다. 고칠 이유가 없으면 그대로 둔다."
- Remove or override vendor E-7 and the E-2 "명사형 종결을 섞음".
- [R] Check whether the 해라체 instruction text ("…윤문기다") pulls output toward 해라체. KatFish shows that generation follows the prompt's level.

To prove the instructions work, measure on the same posts with the old and new prompt (randomised, multiple samples per post):
- Per-sentence voice violations per 100 sentences, by code.
- Graft and revert counts.
- Share of sentences changed.
- Idempotence: a second pass on output, where the target is near-zero edits and zero violations.
- A blinded native-reader rating of 말투 sameness and naturalness. None exists yet.

### 6. Owner's blog (28 posts, 1,627 segments; aggregates only)

**Caveat (owner decision 3):** these posts are the 윤문 엔진's output, not raw writing. They describe the published target style. Uniform 합쇼체 may be partly an artifact of that rewrite.

[M] What the published posts show:
- All 28 posts are HAPSYO-dominant; dominant share median 0.90, minimum 0.70.
- Every post contains 해요체 sentences.
- In body text, 해요체 is mostly imperative CTAs (70) and questions. The leading ending is '세요' (122).
- 20/28 posts contain imperatives; 26/28 contain -시-; 19/28 contain humble 드리다.

Blog-dependent conclusions, all valid only as "published style":
- The mixed 합쇼+해요 CTA pattern must be preserved.
- The vendor misses 해요↔합니다 drift on these posts (the vendor result is valid either way).
- The idempotence control: 0 flags on unchanged re-processing.

The classifier accuracy on blog sentences does not depend on authorship: labels concern form. Human controls remain the KatFish human essays and news.

### 7. Recommendations for v2

1. Port `voice.py` (regex backend) and `gate.py` to the runtime gate, in shadow mode first. Make hard codes trigger repair, then retry or rollback.
2. Run repair (graft, restore, revert) before the existing gates.
3. Replace the TS `sentenceSpeechLevel` with the 8-level classifier. Use `documentRegister` only for the prompt.
4. Drop vendor E-7, E-2's "명사형 종결", and the vendor `check_register`.
5. Add banded alignment (perf), dialect and 하오/하게 stem lists, and a test that pins the pair sets.
