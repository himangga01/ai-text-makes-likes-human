## 2. Rules by research area
**Phonology and orthography**
- Particle allomorph after digits, Latin letters and brackets is listed as an error in the NIKL 2015 diagnosis rubric (10km를, (TF)을). v1 already repairs it.
- Add a hint: a direct quotation followed by 고 should take 라고 ("'…'고 밝혔다" → "'…'라고 밝혔다").
- Spacing errors in native writing concentrate in two places: 본용언+보조용언 (40.5% of 1,268 errors in 이경미 2018) and N+N (21.8%). Both are zones where 한글 맞춤법 permits either spelling.
  - Rule: never "fix" a permitted variant.
  - Spacing is also the most prompt-resistant AI-likeness signal: prompting closed only 13% of the gap (Park et al. 2026). Keep the post-processing conservative.
  - The kornorms article text was not opened, so verify it before coding.
- Normalise Hangul Compatibility Jamo before any edit diff (KoGEC: up to 3.12 BLEU).

**Morphology**
- Speech-level ending classes are closed: 합쇼, 해요, 해, 한다, -음/-함, ellipsis. Preserve each sentence's class.
  - 이유기 2011: 해체 is pragmatically unmarked and differs from 해요체 by -요.
  - 장경현 2013, 김가연 2017, 이정복 2002: -음/-함 and ellipsis deliberately neutralise politeness.
  - 이민영 2026: 반존대 (mixing levels) is functional.
- -시-: the research community is split.
  - 목정수 2017: most -세요 honours the addressee as subject; in a survey, 64.7% judged 사물 존칭 unnatural.
  - 이정복 2024 and 안주호 2022: -시- is shifting toward hearer honorification; -실게요 is accepted.
  - NIKL (2025): there is no regulation on 압존법.
  - Tiering:
    - Tier A, never touch: "선생님 시간 있으세요".
    - Tier B, flag only: "커피 나오셨습니다".
    - Tier C, opt-in fix: first-person -시-, or -시- inside a modal frame.
  - Pedagogy conflict: 정언학 2014 tells students to lower "선배님이 ~하셨다". The engine must not do this.
- Double passive:
  - NIKL Q&A 310695 and 308572: 보여지다 and 잊혀지다 are double passives; 느껴지다 and 남겨지다 are not.
  - Q&A 317346: stacked passives are "likely unnatural", not ungrammatical.
  - -게 되다 is an auxiliary (Q&A 309871) with five or more functions over 84,821 Sejong tokens (김문기·신인환 2023).
  - Rule: closed list, suggestion or opt-in only.
  - Never shorten a long causative: short causatives and passives share a form (김정우 2011, fn. 55).
- -적 has five meanings (이경보 2020) and its frequency is genre-dependent (남길임 2009). Deletion can change meaning.
- -었었-: suggest only, and only when there is no past-before-past contrast.

**Syntax**
- 호응 is the largest non-spelling error class:
  - 방영심·이혜용 2013, cited in 정언학 2014: 33%, ahead of word choice 22%, particles and endings 19%, translationese 9%.
  - 김혜정 외 2016 (749 texts): after spelling (27%), the order is endings, particles, constituent ellipsis, 호응.
  - 이관규 2005: the typical native failure is over-ellipsis of the subject in chained clauses, and the repair adds a constituent.
- Rules:
  - (a) Single-clause topic head + verb predicate flag.
  - (b) Adverb–ending mismatch for 왜냐하면, 비록, 만약 and the negative-polarity adverbs of 임유종 2007. Exclude 전혀 다르다/새롭다.
  - (c) An introduced-only verifier: the output must not create new violations of (a) or (b).
  - (d) When repairing ellipsis, add a constituent only if its referent is recoverable from the previous clause; otherwise flag.
- Never turn an agentless passive into an active (김지혜 2019); it would require inventing an agent. A 에 의해 passive with an explicit agent may be suggested.
- Aspect: -고 있다 is progressive or resultative, and -어 있다 is resultative (박종후 2016, 국어학 77; 송창선 2012). The count of -고 있-/-어 있- per sentence must not drop.

**Semantics**
- Explicitation is meaning addition. 김정우 2011 (264k eojeols): 44.5% of 때문, 33.3% of 그러나 and 20.8% of 하지만 were inserted by translators.
  - Gate: no new causal or contrast connective without a source counterpart.
- KLUE-STS treats politeness function words as "unimportant", and 3.0 counts as a paraphrase. An STS score is blind to speech level and to dropped qualifiers.
- Discourse markers carry stance: 뭐 is about 40% more frequent in monologue (남길임·차지현 2010).
- -들 depends on animacy and definiteness (정경숙 2011), so removing it is not neutral.

**Text and discourse**
- Connective adverbs: 2.87 per 100 words in speech vs 0.93 in writing, with distinct spoken and written lists (한송화 2013).
  - No cross-register swap (그래서 → 따라서).
  - The count of sentence-initial connectives must not increase.
- Cohesion devices do not track coherence (박혜진·이미혜 2017), so never reward adding them.
- Spoken-feature inventory (이해영 2006): use it as a register detector so the engine does not push text toward written register.
- Sentence length:
  - No peer-reviewed numeric norm exists. The figures found are an unsourced 50–70-character newspaper rule and a textbook mean of 37.3 chars per sentence (SD 11.5; n = 9,945).
  - Better-rated writers had longer T-units: 17.33 vs 14.30, p = .017, n = 53 (정희모·김성희 2008).
  - 10–15% of sentences in novels exceed 2x the mean complexity (정대영 2015).
  - Rule: length only selects sentences for a 호응 check. It never triggers a split.

## 3. What the 윤문 엔진 gets wrong or misses (quick-rules.md)
1. It has no 호응 or 비문 category at all (grep finds nothing). The main native error class goes unaddressed.
2. E-7 ("…혼재 → 격식 등급 하나로 일관 유지") contradicts the evidence that mixing and -음/-함 are deliberate. Drop it.
3. E-2 ("'읽고 있다' → '읽는다'") changes aspect. Drop it.
4. E-1 (impose short and long sentences in every paragraph) has no length norm to rest on. Drop it.
5. A-8 lumps "~지게 된다" with double passive. Narrow it to the closed list.
6. A-1/A-2/A-5/A-7/A-9 (에 대해, 를 통해, 관련하여, 가지고 있다, 에 의해) are graded S1 or S2.
   - 최희경 2016: 통하다 occurs 84.4 per 50k eojeols in non-translated news vs 42.1 in translated news; 로부터 is 23.1 in non-translated vs 38.5 in translated vs 33.3 in Sejong.
   - 김순영 2012 calls such forms "not wrong in grammar or meaning".
   - Make them suggest-only.
7. F-5 ("전략적 함의 → 전략 함의") is a meaning risk. Make it essay-only and suggest-only.
8. Nothing forbids adding connectives, which is the documented explicitation failure.
9. The playbook's "블로그 | 친근한 어조, 질문형" pushes a register; the owner's sample opens in 합쇼체.
10. There is no -시- model at all.

## 4. Fifteen most actionable findings, ranked by expected impact [R]
1. **Per-sentence ending-class gate.** Never unify levels; drop E-7.
   - Evidence: 이민영 2026; 이유기 2011; 장경현 2013; 김가연 2017; LoCar 2026 (LLM honorific control "unstable"); 정준녕 2021 (24–33% Bad despite BLEU 66.7); KLUE-STS politeness-blind.
   - Component: verifier.
2. **-시- count per predicate is invariant.** Tiers A/B/C; no 압존법 change; no honorific lowering.
   - Evidence: 목정수 2017 (full text); 이정복 2024; 안주호 2022; NIKL 압존법 answer 2025; Noh et al. 2024; 정언학 2014.
   - Component: verifier and diagnosis.
3. **Length never triggers an edit; keep sentence boundaries.** Drop E-1.
   - Evidence: 정언학 2014; 최소영 2024; 정희모·김성희 2008; 정대영 2015; [M-ours] blog median 48 chars.
   - Component: prompt and verifier.
4. **Explicitation gate.** No new 때문, 그러나 or 따라서; no connective register swap; connective count does not increase.
   - Evidence: 김정우 2011 and 2012; 한송화 2013; 박혜진·이미혜 2017; 이수곤 2021.
   - Component: verifier.
5. **Aspect guard** for -고 있-/-어 있-. Drop E-2.
   - Evidence: 박종후 2016; 송창선 2012.
   - Component: meaning guard (new axis).
6. **호응 diagnosis plus an introduced-only 호응 check,** scoped to single-clause sentences.
   - Evidence: 방영심·이혜용 via 정언학; 김혜정 2016; NIKL 2015; 이관규 2005; 곽수진·김영주 2010; [M-ours] 1.89 vs 0.04 per 10k, precision 4/4 and 4/5 in essays, about 1/13 in news, 0 on the blog.
   - Component: diagnosis and verifier.
7. **Minimal-edit prompt contract** ("return unchanged if natural; keep meaning, tone and structure; only the listed edit classes"), and measure over-editing on our own data.
   - Evidence: Türker & Eryiğit 2026; Fang 2023; Maeng 2023 (Korean GPT-4 precision 0.44 vs baseline 0.33; expression-choice accuracy only 20–52%).
   - Component: prompt and evaluation.
8. **Translationese items stay suggest-only.**
   - Evidence: 최희경 2016; 김순영 2012; 김정우 2012; 최서영 2022; 정경숙 2011; [M-ours].
   - Component: diagnostics.
9. **Protected voice tokens:** ㅎㅎ, ^^, ~, "...", -음/-함, hedges such as 것 같다, and markers such as 뭐, 사실, 진짜.
   - Evidence: 박은하 2018; 이정복 2002; 남길임·차지현 2010; 이해영 2006.
   - Component: protection layer and verifier.
10. **Double passive: closed list, suggestion only.** Exclude 느껴지다, 남겨지다, 여겨지다 and -게 되다. Never shorten causatives.
    - Evidence: NIKL Q&A 310695, 308572, 317346, 309871; 김문기·신인환 2023; [M-ours] rare, and human and LLM rates are equal.
    - Component: post-processing.
11. **Evaluation protocol:** multi-reference gold; ERRANT-style Korean edit classes with per-class budgets; counts per 1,000 syllables; spell checkers are not an oracle; no evaluation on synthetic noise alone; LLM-judged ending naturalness is never a hard gate.
    - Evidence: KoLLA 2025 (2 refs, κ 0.82); NIKL 2015; 김영일·김완섭 2021 (checkers catch 3–5 of 30 sentence-level errors); 구선민 2021; KoSEnd (α 0.30–0.75).
    - Component: T7 harness.
12. **Spacing: fix only unambiguous violations** and follow the writer's own convention.
    - Evidence: 이경미 2018; 남수경 2025; Park et al. 2026.
    - Component: post-processing.
13. **NIKL rubric items as low-risk suggestions** (라고/고, 되어지다, transitive-base 시키다, 중에 있다, 필요로 하다), with the rubric's exception list as false-positive guards.
    - Evidence: NIKL 2015 (full text); [M-ours] 0.60 vs 0.22.
    - Component: diagnostics and josa module.
14. **No automatic -적 deletion or 순화어 replacement; match terms on morphemes, not substrings.**
    - Evidence: 이경보 2020; 남길임 2009; 백경미 2022; MOIS 2018 (false positive 산하기관→"하기→아래"); [M-ours] AUROC 0.81 vs 0.51.
    - Component: diagnostics; limit F-5.
15. **Optional edit-consensus gate** (k samples, keep edits at or above a threshold τ) for high-risk chunks only.
    - Evidence: Goto 2026 (Korean F0.5: gemma +2.7, Llama +0.9, Qwen −2.4).
    - Component: experimental gate.

## 6. Recommendations for v2 by component
- **Verifier.** Add new gates:
  - ending class per sentence
  - -시- count per predicate
  - -고 있-/-어 있- count
  - connective set difference and register
  - protected-token multiset
  - sentence type (statement/question/command)
  - introduced-only 호응

  Ship them in shadow mode first.
- **Diagnosis.**
  - Add a 호응 and ellipsis family, scoped to single clauses.
  - Make translationese, -적, double passive and 필요로 하다 suggest-only, with counterexamples.
  - Never emit these hints: shorten, unify level, remove hedge, remove ellipsis, add connective, purify loanword.
- **Prompt.**
  - Use the minimal-edit contract with the allowed edit classes.
  - Add: "Do not add 때문/그러나/따라서", "keep -고 있다", "keep -시- and every sentence's ending".
- **Post-processing.**
  - Add the 라고/고 hint.
  - Apply the closed double-passive suggestion list.
  - Fix spacing only for unambiguous violations.
- **Evaluation (with T7).**
  - Use multi-reference pairs and per-class edit counts.
  - Build test items from the NIKL 2015 examples, the 새국어생활 minimal pairs (김순영 2012, 최인호 2016, 이관규 2005), honorific tiers A/B/C and aspect pairs.
  - Normalise counts per 1,000 syllables.
  - Blinded native-reader judgements are still required.
- **Vendor rules.**
  - Drop E-1, E-2 and E-7.
  - Narrow A-8.
  - Demote A-1, A-2, A-5, A-7, A-9 and F-5 to suggest-only.
