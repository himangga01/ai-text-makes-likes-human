## 0. Conclusions

1. **The NIKL surveys cannot rank types of awkwardness.** [S] Neither the 언어 의식 questionnaire nor the 국어능력 test asks which awkward constructions readers notice. They support only broad statements:
   - Adult writing ability is the weakest domain: 11.2% reach level 4 and 21.9% are at level 1.
   - Norm knowledge is weak in recognition tests: items on spelling, standard forms and loanwords were answered correctly by 43–65% of 5,000 adults.
   - For public documents the top complaint is long, hard-to-understand sentences (50.1%), far above spelling and norm errors (15.2%).
   Do not cite these surveys as evidence for any specific rewrite rule.
2. **NIKL's own expert-edit data shows a different frequency profile from the "LLM tell" lists.** [S] In 304,046 expert edits over 5,031 public documents:
   - Spelling, spacing and punctuation make up 50.7% of cases, and spacing alone 41.4%.
   - Omission 10.3%, connectives 6.0%, particles 3.0%.
   - Passive and causative only 1.0%, agreement and tense 0.7%, word order 0.65%, honorifics 0.19%.
   This is public-sector prose, not blog prose.
3. **The one mechanically checkable norm family I measured is almost perfectly human-versus-LLM separating, and it is not an LLM tell.** [M] A glued dependent noun (할수 있다, 먹을것) or a glued "-지 않" occurs at:
   - 0.17 per 10k Hangul characters in professional news (n=1,500 articles, 1.04M characters).
   - 26.8 per 10k in K-12 student essays (n=181).
   - 0 in 4 LLM generators (n=590 essays, 392k characters).
   - **0 in the owner's 28 public blog posts (59,392 characters).**
   Spelling and spacing repair is a human-norm layer. For this owner it has almost nothing to fix.
4. **A whole-sentence re-spacing checker (Kiwi `space()`) is unusable as a detector.** [M] It flags 84.2% of news sentences, versus 67.5% of student-essay sentences and 42.0% of GPT-4o essay sentences. Compound nouns, auxiliary verbs and proper nouns are optional-space cases under 맞춤법 arts. 46–50.
5. **NIKL's consultation service (온라인가나다) rarely issues a firm "this is wrong, write X".** [S] In my purposive sample of 34 items, only 5 end that way, and all 5 are spelling or agreement (호응) breaks. 11 items say explicitly "not an error", 6 give conditional advice, and 10 decline. NIKL also states that 윤문 and review of AI corrections are outside its scope.
6. **The 모두의 말뭉치 holds several corpora that are directly useful for evaluation.** [S] Highest value:
   - The 2024 instruction corpus: 1,100 expert 표현 corrections, 950 of them sentence-level, each tagged with an error type.
   - The 2025 Naver-blog subset: 73,176 posts.
   - The 유사 문장 (paraphrase) corpus.
   - The 국어 지식 기초 자료 (valid, allowed and exception forms).
   The terms permit AI product evaluation by approved application, but they forbid redistribution and require prior NIKL approval before publishing results. A commercial-service use has to be confirmed in writing. NIKL data must stay out of git.
7. **The 다듬은 말 database (18,320 entries) cannot be used as a flat lexicon.** [M] Naively it fires on 100% of documents. Even filtered to loanword-origin entries it fires on 82% of news articles and 82% of the owner's posts. Many entries are sense-specific, for example 서비스 → "(운동 경기에서) 메기기". It does not separate human text from LLM text.
8. **The 윤문 엔진 has no norm layer at all.** `vendor/.../sanitize_text.py` states "맞춤법·띄어쓰기·문장부호를 고치지 않는다". The BN/VX spacing rules are on a hold list in `ai-tell-taxonomy.md` line 892. The only NIKL citation in its taxonomy is the `~의` caveat C5. It also has no closed error taxonomy with a "no error" exit.

## 1. Surveys: what Korean adults can and cannot do

### 1.1 국민의 국어능력 실태 조사, 3rd round (2023–2025)

Source: NIKL press release of 2026-02-06 [S, opened] and the 2024 and 2025 reports [S, PDFs opened]. All are 공공누리 type 1 (attribution only).

- **Design.** 20–69-year-olds in 17 provinces, one-to-one visits. 2024 covered reading and 문법·규범 with n=5,000. 2025 covered writing with n=3,000 plus a 50-person group-administered comparison sample. Two trained raters scored each essay.
- **Level distribution** (4 = excellent, 1 = below basic; 2025 report abstract):

| Domain | L4 | L3 | L2 | L1 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listening | 40.6 | 27.7 | 25.7 | 6.0 |
| Reading | 33.0 | 28.5 | 24.9 | 13.7 |
| 문법·규범 | 29.6 | 37.3 | 23.5 | 9.6 |
| Speaking | 18.1 | 29.8 | 32.3 | 19.9 |
| **Writing** | **11.2** | 26.4 | 40.5 | **21.9** |

- **Subgroup effects.** Writing at L4 is 3.9% for below-high-school education versus 13.9% for college and above. Gender is not significant. Public or obligatory writing experience correlates with higher writing scores. The generative-AI-use variable had no significant predictive power in the 2025 decision-tree analysis (report p. 10884 of the text dump; abstract only checked).
- **문법·규범 item cards** (2024 report, PDF pp. 138–152; share correct in a 4-option multiple-choice test, n=5,000):

| Item | Evaluation element | Share correct |
| --- | --- | --- |
| G-1 | modality | 0.85 |
| G-2 | sentence-component agreement (호응) | 0.86 |
| G-6 | particles and endings | 0.87 |
| G-4 | Sino-Korean vocabulary | 0.91 |
| G-7 | discourse marker | 0.64 |
| G-3 | deixis | 0.69 |
| G-8 | spelling by pronunciation | 0.48 |
| G-9, G-10 | spelling by form | 0.65 and 0.55 |
| G-11 | multiple standard forms | 0.46 |
| G-12 | standard pronunciation | 0.51 |
| G-13 | loanword spelling | 0.43 |

- **The writing rubric** (2025 report, Tables II-24 to II-27, PDF pp. 70–76). The 표현 criterion deducts for these faults:
  - 호응 errors;
  - three or more context-inappropriate words or misspellings;
  - ending errors: a sentence left unfinished, or 종결어미 불일치 (mixed endings);
  - extreme sentence length, either a run-on list or fragments.
  Raters were told that "주어나 목적어가 빠지는 등의 심각한 비문이 아니라면 어색한 표현은 어느 정도 허용적으로 채점함" (PDF pp. 75–76): awkwardness short of serious ungrammaticality is graded permissively.
- **Inter-rater reliability** (Cohen's κ, Table II-29): 표현 is the lowest criterion on every item, at 0.72, 0.63, 0.58 and 0.51. 내용 and 조직 range from 0.55 to 0.87. Even trained raters agree least about language-surface errors.

**Implications for v2:**
- [R] Recognising a 호응 or particle error is easy (0.85–0.87) and writing is hard. The failures that raters deduct for (호응, unfinished sentences, ending mismatch) are the ungrammatical class that principle 2 targets. Sentence-final mismatch is also a 말투 concern for T2.
- [R, evaluation item] Plan for modest agreement on any "awkwardness" label. Use at least 3 raters with adjudication, and report κ per defect type.
- [R] NIKL's raters tolerate mere awkwardness, which supports "no edit is a valid result".

### 1.2 국민의 언어 의식 조사 2025 (5th wave since 2005)

Source: report page `https://korean.go.kr/front/reportData/reportDataView.do?mn_id=&searchOrder=years&report_seq=1209&pageIndex=1` and its PDF [S, opened]. Licence: type 1 plus the "AI유형". The AI type allows use as AI training data, commercial or not, provided that substantially similar outputs are prevented, direct quotations are attributed, and derived training data is not resold. Design: 5,000 adults aged 20–69, face-to-face household interviews.

- **Perceived correctness.** Only 13.5% say Koreans use Korean incorrectly, 38.6% say "average", and 48.0% say correctly (Table 3-4, p. 66 of the PDF).
- **Why they say it is incorrect** (n=671, Table 3-5). The 2025 questionnaire added a new option, which shifts the comparison with 2020:
  - inappropriate vocabulary (slang, neologisms): 39.0%;
  - spelling and spacing: 27.1% (over 40% in 2020);
  - weak connection of content: 10.0%;
  - limited word use: 9.4%;
  - inappropriate honorifics: 7.6%;
  - pronunciation or intonation: 6.9%.
- **Self-rating:** 46.0% say they follow the orthography norms, 10.9% say they do not (p. 182), down from 2020.
- **Public-agency language, top priorities for improvement** (Table 3-68, two choices per person):
  - 50.1% long, complex, hard-to-understand sentences;
  - 44.1% and 40.6% for unnecessary foreign words and hard words. The report's own text orders these two differently on p. 54/198 versus p. 257, so I do not rely on their order;
  - 31.3% authoritative or high-handed expressions;
  - **15.2% spelling and norm violations**;
  - 8.4% discriminatory expressions.
- **Open data:** the earlier 2022 results are on data.go.kr as XLSX under "이용허락범위 제한 없음" (`https://www.data.go.kr/data/15119445/fileData.do`, opened).

**Implications:**
- It is perception data. The answer options contain no awkwardness category (호응, passive, translationese).
- It supports clarity of long sentences as the main reader complaint for public prose (T4's area) and ranks spelling and spacing low.
- It lends no evidence to "reduce translationese".

## 2. NIKL expert-edit data: 공공언어 감수 자료

Source: *공공언어 감수 자료 구축 방안 연구* (양명희 et al., 2023) [S, full PDF opened]. URL: `https://www.korean.go.kr/front/reportData/reportDataView.do?mn_id=45&report_seq=1137`. Licence: **type 4 (attribution, no commercial use, no derivatives)**, so it may be read and cited but not ingested into a product.

- **Content.** 5,031 public documents (notices, press releases, and expert-revised documents from 2010–2022), giving 304,046 span-level before/after cases. Each case has a source span, a fixed expression and one of 16 error types. Quality control: 28.3% of documents were re-checked, and every case passed a format check.
- **Share of cases** (Table 2, PDF p. 27):
  - Orthography 50.7%: spacing 41.4%, punctuation 7.8%, 맞춤법/표준어 1.0%, loanword spelling 0.5%.
  - Grammar 36.4%: 문법상의 표현 오류 13.7%, omission 10.3%, connectives 6.0%, particles 3.0%, passive/causative 1.0%, endings 0.84%, 호응/시제 0.7%, word order 0.65%, honorifics 0.19%.
  - Vocabulary 12.9%: foreign/loanword overuse 6.9%, word choice 6.0%.
- **Annotator policy that matters.**
  - The team did **not** treat noun+noun spacing as an error where the permitted-variant articles apply (PDF pp. 14–15). Their reason: finding "어색한 문장과 표현" is more useful for an automatic rewriting tool than policing optional spacing.
  - Original and permitted forms are decided by period. The 2010–16 portion favours the principle form (하여야). The 2017–22 portion accepts 해야.
- **Model result.** The authors fine-tuned BERT span detectors (F1 0.96–0.98, PDF p. 38) and then say it is "그 자체로 상용화하기는 어렵다" (PDF p. 40). The F1 is dominated by the O label, and the I-label F1 is about 0.55. Do not cite those figures as detector quality.
- **Distribution of this dataset is unverified.** It is not in the 모두의 말뭉치 list I parsed. A related set, the 2024 국어 지식 기초 자료, is built from "공문서 감수 자료 및 국어생활 상담 자료".

**Implications:**
- [R] Frequency is not harm. If v2 ranks rules by frequency in NIKL editing, spacing and punctuation dominate. These are low-value, voice-neutral and often optional-variant cases for a blog rewriter. Passive and causative are only 1.0%, so a "needless passive" rule needs a harm argument, not a frequency one.
- [R, evaluation item] Report per-type fire rates against these shares when the 2024 instruction corpus (section 3) is used.

## 3. Norms, dictionaries and what is mechanically checkable

**Norm text I opened** (한글 맞춤법, `https://www.korean.go.kr/kornorms/regltn/popup/regltnNtfcView.do?ntfc_hist_no=1001&ntfc_no=6&regltn_code=0001`; quotes via the page):
- Art. 42: dependent nouns are spaced.
- Art. 43: unit nouns are spaced, but attaching to a numeral is allowed.
- Art. 46: consecutive one-syllable words may be attached.
- Art. 47: auxiliary verbs are spaced in principle, and attaching is allowed.
- Arts. 49–50: proper nouns and technical terms are spaced by word in principle, and attaching by unit is allowed.
- Art. 53: the ending is written -(으)ㄹ게, not -(으)ㄹ께.

**Decision patterns:**
- **Firm answers** only for spelling tables and agreement breaks (subject, object or predicate mismatch, a missing required component). These come with 1–2 explicit repairs.
- **Overlap or redundancy (겹말)** is never called an error. Typical wording: "다소 겹쳐 보일 수 있으나 단정하기 어렵다", or morpheme-type reasoning (a lexical word and a grammatical particle do not overlap).
- **Translationese and passives** are not labelled errors. 331412 reads: "일본어 번역투… 오류나 오자, 잘못이라고 단정할 수는 없습니다 … 일본어 번역투를 회피하는 맥락에서는 쓰지 않는 것이 좋을 것". 327439 adds that a passive with a non-agent subject is fine and overuse is "문체적 문제".
- **Register and honorifics:** mixing 해요체 and a formal ending for one addressee is "흔히 나타난다"; honorific scope for objects is "명확히 정해져 있지 않다"; workplace announcement honorifics are "어문 규범에서 다루지 않는다 … 소속된 기관의 관습을 따라".
- **Naturalness verdicts are refused.** Typical: "문맥이나 사용 환경, 개인의 언어 감각 등에 따라 달라질 수 있어 단정적으로 답변해 드리기 어렵습니다."
- **333920 (a user asked about Gemini's correction):** "AI 답변의 오류에 대한 판단이나 해석, 윤문 등은 온라인가나다의 답변 범위를 벗어납니다."

**Implications:**
- [R] Map rules to NIKL authority levels: NORM, NIKL-ADVICE (soft), CONTESTED, NONE. Never tell the writer that "NIKL says this is more natural".
- [R] Only classes A may be fixed by default.
- [R] Classes C and E give counterexamples for the lexicon track: 기간 동안, 각 나라별, 수급받다 and the 에 있어서 family stay suggest-only or leave.

## 8. What the 윤문 엔진 gets wrong or misses in this area

- **No norm layer.** `vendor/humanize-engine/scripts/sanitize_text.py` says "맞춤법·띄어쓰기·문장부호를 고치지 않는다". The BN/VX spacing rules are held back (`ai-tell-taxonomy.md` line 892). The only NIKL reference in its taxonomy is the `~의` caveat (C5). It uses nothing from the NIKL error taxonomies above.
- **Its rule set targets LLM tells.** NIKL's own editing frequency profile and consultation answers treat many tells (translationese, passive, 겹말) as "not an error". The existing EVALUATION record already rejected several of them on measurement; the Q&A pages add the authority-level view.
- **No closed taxonomy with a "no error" path.** NIKL's own scaffolding does have one (see D3).
- **For this owner the gap is small.** [M] Zero hits of the measured families in the owner's posts means a norm layer would not change their output. It would matter for other writers (student-level text).

## 9. Recommendations for v2

**D1. Norm layer in three tiers** (rule, trigger, counterexamples, false-positive evidence, genre boundary):
- *Tier A, fix inside already-rewritten sentences:* particle allomorph (exists). Add the glued dependent noun 수/것/줄/리 after an adnominal ending, a glued "-지 않/못하", and a reviewed closed list of never-valid misspellings. Evidence: 18+2+1 hits in 1.04M news characters; all 10 inspected contexts genuine. Exclusions: hashtags, URLs, quotes, headings, code, titles, and 바/데. Genre boundary: not for verse or deliberately fused styles.
- *Tier B, report only:* the same patterns outside rewritten sentences; 안/않 and 되/돼 in unambiguous contexts; loanword spelling for words in the 70,800-example data; the 30선 lexical items.
- *Tier C, never:* noun+noun, proper-noun or technical-term spacing; 보조용언 spacing; unit and numeral spacing; 복수 표준어; sound-based colloquial spellings; punctuation style; brand names; dialect.

**D2. Introduced-only gate for Tier A patterns**, like the josa gate: reject a candidate that introduces a Tier A violation the source did not have. [M] The four LLMs produced 0 such hits, so the gate costs nothing and protects against weaker local models.

**D3. Prompt scaffolding from the NIKL 2024 instruction corpus.** Its persona/task design is: task 1 names the error type from a closed 25-type list and says "문장에 나타난 오류가 <오류 유형>에 없다면 <오류 유형>은 따로 제시하지 마세요"; task 2 cites the norm or rule; task 3 gives a fixed-format minimal rewrite. Adopt the closed taxonomy, the explicit "none" exit and the rule-then-edit order (a design choice; NIKL does not show it is better). The 25 types include 부적절한 높임법, 어순, 조사, 피동, 사동, four 호응 types, 지나치게 긴 수식어, three connection types, 종결어미, 대용 표현, 필요한 문장 성분 생략, 시제.

**D4. A four-way decision policy** from section 5: FIX (norm tables, 호응 breaks, missing components), SUGGEST (passive, causative, translationese, 겹말), LEAVE (register mixing, honorific scope, optional-space cases), DEFER (ambiguous scope, 중의성). Attach an authority tag to each rule so prompts never claim NIKL backing for suggestions.

**D5. Priority by harm, not frequency.** Use section 2's distribution only to size evaluation cells. The rubric items (호응 errors, unfinished sentences, ending mismatch, extreme sentence length) are the defensible "ungrammatical" class.

**D6. Evaluation items to build from NIKL data** (kept outside git):
- E1: the 2024 표현 subset gives fix accuracy plus an unrelated-edit count outside the error span.
- E2: unedited sentences from the same essays plus news or blog sentences give the edit rate on natural text, which should be near zero.
- E3: the 유사 문장 human paraphrases give guard over-blocking on natural sentences, independent of the in-house 555 pairs.
- E4: the 국어 지식 기초 자료 allowed and exception rows give the false-fix rate on valid variants.
- E5: the 2025 Naver-blog subset gives per-rule fire rates in the owner's genre, which replaces the 28-post frame with n=73,176.
- E6: human rating with at least 3 raters, citing the 표현 κ range 0.51–0.72 as the reliability expectation.

**D7. Do not wire these into the product:**
- 다듬은 말 as an automatic lexicon;
- the full 우리말샘/표준국어대사전 text (share-alike and attribution obligations);
- any NIKL corpus text in the repository (no redistribution).
