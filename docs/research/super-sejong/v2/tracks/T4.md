## 0. Bottom line

1. **No official Korean source gives a numeric clarity threshold.** NIKL, 법제처, Toss and ISO 24495-1 all give principles. NIKL's own diagnosis scores sentence length as a human 1–5 Likert item. Thresholds therefore have to come from genre-matched corpora.
2. **Only 2–3 of the ~20 mechanical clarity checks I built reach usable precision on hand-checked samples.**
   - The usable ones are: a genre-calibrated long multi-clause sentence flag, the double-approximation fix (약 N … 가량), and acronym/배 hints.
   - Most "clarity" defects (scope ambiguity, reference ambiguity, hedging, noun strings, nested quotes) cannot be found by syntax alone. Their precision was 0–16%.
3. **On the owner's blog the correct clarity action is usually no edit.**
   - 28 posts, 1,435 non-heading sentences.
   - Only 12 sentences (0.84%) have ≥28 eojeol, and I judged 6 of those worth splitting.
   - Double negation, nested quotes and percent/배 ambiguity produced 0 hits in the whole blog.
4. **The 윤문 엔진 has two clarity-harming rules.**
   - E-1 builds long sentences by merging adjacent ones.
   - A-18 triggers on about half of all sentences.
   - It has no clarity-regression gate (details in section 5).
5. **Recommendation:**
   - Treat clarity mainly as a non-regression guard on edits plus an evaluation rubric.
   - Allow one LLM-assisted targeted action (split a flagged long sentence under constraints) and one deterministic edit (double approximation).
   - Everything else is a hint or rubric-only.

Evidence levels used below: **[M]** measured on our data, **[S]** read in a source I opened, **[R]** my reasoning.

**Proposed review trigger** [R, calibrated on [M]]
- Fire when a sentence is ≥ max(28, the P99 of the declared genre's human reference) eojeol and has ≥4 clause endings. The initial values would be about 28 for blog/essay-like text and about 40 for abstract/news.

## 5. What the 윤문 엔진 gets wrong or misses [M] (rules quoted from `quick-rules.md`)

- **E-1** ("문장 길이 균일 + 100자+ 장문 부재 → 인접 문장을 이어 만든 장문 1개를 문단마다").
  - It is a clarity-harming rule. NIKL ⑬ says one idea per sentence, and my hand-check shows long multi-clause sentences are burdensome in 72% of the student-essay sample.
  - Its second condition ("no 100+ char sentence") holds for 96.1% of the owner's multi-sentence paragraphs (174/181), and 12 of 28 posts (43%) have no ≥100-char sentence at all.
  - On this blog it would therefore try to build a long sentence in almost every paragraph.
  - The uniformity condition was not measured. If it is common in the blog, E-1 fires almost everywhere.
- **A-18** ("명사 앞 3어절 이상 관형구/관계절 → 분리").
  - My approximation (an adnominal clause of ≥3 eojeol) matches 46.9% of blog sentences and 47–79% in all other corpora.
  - The denser, genuinely hard version (≥4 ETM in ≥14 eojeol) has 16% precision.
  - Applied as stated, A-18 pushes the writer to rework about half the owner's sentences. That contradicts "keep voice, fix only awkward Korean".
- **A-16** (count antecedent candidates before touching a demonstrative) has the right design: unclear means keep. My measurement shows true ambiguity is rare (0/25), so it should almost never act.
- **No clarity-regression gate.** A grep of `verify_gates.py` and `checks.py` for sentence-length terms found nothing. `metrics_v2.py` has passive-count and pronoun-density metrics, but the earlier evaluation recorded these as noise.
- **Nothing for acronym gloss, "N배", agentless obligation or fused-CTA sentences.**
- **Sound as written:** G-1/G-2 ("단정 전환 금지") are correct. My hedge data supports not touching hedges.

## 6. Recommendations for v2

**Tier A: diagnose then targeted edit** (small)
1. **LONG_REVIEW** (section 4 trigger), executed as an LLM span request.
   - The request says: "split into 2–3 sentences; keep every proposition, number, name and condition; use the document's dominant speech level for the new sentence endings; add no new connectives or CTA; if the CTA clause is fused, keep its wording unchanged."
   - Verify afterwards:
     - Sentence count rose by the expected amount.
     - Every new sentence ends in the document's level (voice-lock gate, T2).
     - No bare demonstrative or new acronym appears.
     - No new sentence exceeds the trigger.
     - The existing meaning guards pass.
   - Skip the request when the sentence is a quotation.
2. **APPROX2 fix** (deterministic). Delete the trailing 가량/정도/쯤/안팎/내외 when it follows 약/대략/거의 + N + unit. Meaning, honorifics and ending are unchanged.
   - Deleting the trailing marker rather than the leading 약 is my design choice; no reader study backs it.
   - Prevalence is 0.12 per 10k chars in news and 0 in the blog, so this is low value.

**Tier B: hint to the LLM, no edit**
- Unglossed acronym (never invent an expansion).
- 배 increase without a base.
- Litotes double negation.
- Agentless obligation passive.
- Hard word, with an exact-morpheme lexicon only.

**Tier C: evaluation rubric only**
- Modifier scope.
- Demonstrative reference.
- Nested quotes.
- Noun strings.
- Dense relative clauses.
- Hedge and intensifier stacking.
- List parallelism.
- Comparison ambiguity.
- Percent vs percentage-point.

**Tier D: drop**
- A-18 as an editing rule.
- E-1 for clarity purposes.
- Bare-numeral.
- Comma-series.
- Voice-shift (T3's domain).

**Clarity non-regression guard** (the main v2 value, [R]). Compare source and candidate sentence by sentence and block or retry when any of these holds:
- The candidate creates a sentence longer than the source's longest aligned sentence by more than 2 eojeol, or a new sentence above the trigger. This forbids E-1-style merging.
- It adds sentence-initial bare demonstratives.
- It adds agentless obligation passives.
- It adds an acronym without gloss, or drops an existing gloss.
- It changes any numeral's adjacent unit or marker (배, %p, 이상/이하).
- It removes a hedge or intensifier (meaning-guard overlap).
- It turns a "모든 …는 것은 아니다" partial negation into total negation.
- It changes list markers or line structure.

I could not measure the false-block rate of these guards, because no source/rewrite pairs exist. The existing 555-pair meaning set and T7's seed set can calibrate it.

**Risks of letting the LLM improve clarity at all:** KatFish shows LLM prose adds 4–5× more bare demonstratives and agentless passives than human prose. A rewrite can lower clarity while appearing polished.

## 7. Clarity rubric (1–5, for blinded human or LLM-judge evaluation)

**Unit.** A passage of 3–8 sentences (about 400 Korean characters at most) from one paragraph. **Reader.** An intended adult reader, a non-expert for the topic. **Basis.** NIKL's own scoring uses 1–5 Likert items with multiple raters [S]. ISO 24495-1 Principle 4 requires evaluation with readers [S, TOC only].

| Score | Anchor (Korean) | Example (my own constructions) |
|---|---|---|
| **5** 한 번에 읽힘 | 누가·무엇을·언제·얼마(조건·수치)가 첫 읽기에 모두 잡히고, 지시어·수식 범위·병렬 구조·단위에 걸리는 곳이 없다. 전문어는 처음 나올 때 풀이된다. | "신청서는 접수일부터 14일 안에 내야 합니다. 기한이 지나면 새로 신청하셔야 합니다." |
| **4** 거의 막힘 없음 | 한 곳에서 잠깐 걸리지만 문맥으로 바로 풀린다. 오해 가능성은 사실상 없다. | "접수 뒤에는 서류 검토와 결격사유 확인을 거쳐 등록증이 발급됩니다." (행위 주체 생략, 독자는 안다) |
| **3** 다시 읽어야 이해됨 | 한 문장에 주장이 둘 이상이거나, 수식 범위·지시 대상·주어가 모호해 앞뒤를 보고 추론해야 한다. 핵심은 파악된다. | "발급 여부를 단정하기보다 안내문과 실제 상황을 바탕으로 자료를 준비하는 데 중점을 두고 있으니 상담이 필요하신 분들은 연락 주시길 바랍니다." (설명과 안내가 한 문장에 융합) |
| **2** 오독 가능 | 서로 다른 두 해석이 모두 성립하거나, 핵심 조건·주체·수치 중 하나가 복원되지 않는다. 독자 상당수가 한 번은 잘못 읽는다. | "매출이 20%에서 25%로 5% 올랐다." (포인트인지 비율인지 모호); "접수 기간 이후 제출한 서류는 제외한 신청자 및 대리인 또는 보호자가 동의한 경우만 인정한다." (누가 제외되는지 불명) |

**Sub-scores** (each 1–5 with the same anchor logic):
- C1 문장 부담·핵심 파악.
- C2 지시·범위·행위 주체.
- C3 수치·단위·용어·약어 풀이.
- C4 구조·병렬·목록.

**Judge output (JSON).** `{C1..C4, overall, evidence_span, source_vs_candidate: better|same|worse}`.

**Protocol**
- Blind source and candidate.
- Randomise A/B order and run both orders, scoring each text independently before the pairwise call.
- Run at least 3 judge samples, and count a defect only when ≥2 samples agree. This mirrors NIKL's "≥2 raters" rule [S].
- Calibrate the LLM judge against at least 60 human-rated passages and report weighted κ, not just agreement.
- Include natural no-edit controls. Their expected Δ is 0.

**Decision rule** [R]
- Δclarity = candidate − source, on a 5-point scale.
- No-harm is defined as the mean Δ on natural controls being ≥ −0.1, with the CI reported.
- An improvement claim needs Δ > 0 with a CI excluding 0 on items with injected clarity defects.
- Only injected defects that are mechanically fixable (run-on, double approximation) should be scored as "repair".
- Track separately a boolean "meaning or 말투 changed", which vetoes any clarity gain.

## 8. Open questions

- **No native-reader labels.** All precision figures come from one LLM annotator and small samples. Several CIs are wide, for example 1/28 has a Wilson CI of 0.6–18%. A native-editor audit of `labels_stable.json` (about 450 labelled hits) is the most valuable next step.
- **Blog sample is small** (28 posts, 1,435 sentences). Blog-only precision cells have n ≤ 10.
- **Student essays** are an unsafe stand-in for adult blog prose. The 72% figure for long sentences describes essays, not the owner.
