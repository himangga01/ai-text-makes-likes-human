## 3. Protocol

### 3.1 Principles that avoid the two earlier problems

- **Power.** Sizes are computed from endpoints (section 5), with an internal pilot (the seed set, about 4 h) to estimate rates, SD and ICC before N is locked.
- **Circularity.**
  1. No arm's gate or detector is an outcome. Outcomes use (a) gold from reversible corruption, (b) a frozen independent audit that no arm imports, (c) blinded judges of a different model family from the writer, (d) a human-rated subset. Replay flags are reported only as "engine self-consistency".
  2. Audit components are validated on hand-labelled probes (72 in the seed), never tuned on the benchmark.
  3. A dev set and a sealed test set. The seed set is dev. The sealed set is written by other authors and run once.
  4. A "gate shoot-out" on identical frozen candidates compares old and new gates without model stochasticity.

### 3.2 Arms, routes and configurations

- **Arms:**
  - A0 = commit c792440, the old engine, via `git archive` into its own directory.
  - A2 = v2 candidate.
  - A1 = 4b6a7a9 (optional, subset).
- **Routes:**
  - Primary: direct-text, strength medium (the owner's real path).
  - Secondary: text-file, normal.
  - The same model and effort for all arms, recorded.
- **Default configuration (owner decisions):**
  - Voice is scored per sentence only (level, ending class, honorific `-시-`/께서/님, humble forms, first-person marker, and quotations verbatim). The document-level register is never a reference.
  - Colloquial spellings (할께-type) and CMC tokens (ㅋㅋ, ㅎㅎ, ^^, ~, ㅠㅠ) are protected tokens, and changing one counts as over-editing.
  - The opt-in `fix_colloquial_spelling` is a separate configuration. It runs only if the option ships, with the opposite expectation on the same items.

### 3.3 Four tiers

- **Tier A (offline, no LLM):**
  - Gates and diagnostics on frozen labelled pairs (the 72 probes).
  - Diagnosis recall and precision on spans.
  - Prompt size.
  - The restore, repair and strip steps on gold text.
- **Tier B (stub writer, offline):**
  - A stub `CodexAdapter` in `HumanizeEngine` returns, in turn, the identity, the gold text, and a damaged gold text.
  - Checks that gold passes through unrolled-back and that damage is caught or rolled back.
- **Tier C (live writer, paired):** same item, both arms, one job at a time, arm order alternating by item hash, R=1 plus R=2 on a 150-item random subset for run variance.
- **Tier D (judges and humans).**

### 3.5 Real-draft sub-studies: what "improvement" means against a no-edit baseline

On the owner's real route A0 is effectively the identity (B0), so I use four parts, no gold needed except where stated.

1. **Spiked drafts (gold).**
   - Apply reversible corruptions to natural drafts: allomorph flips, 되어지다, spacing, 겹말 insertion, 함에 있어서. Gold = the original sentence.
   - Stratify the spikes by the old engine's protected-sentence flag.
   - Metrics: repair rate overall and by protected/unprotected sentence, collateral edits in the other ~600 sentences, and voice, meaning and CTA preservation.
   - The old engine's ceiling is set by design to roughly the unprotected share of sites (about 8–44%).
   - The generator is aggregate-only here. Spiked texts are not persisted by me, since they would copy owner text.
2. **Natural drafts.**
   - Blinded edit-level adjudication of every v2 edit as improvement / neutral / harmful (harm = meaning, voice, CTA, new defect, or protected-token change).
   - Reported as improving edits and harmful edits per 100 sentences, plus edit precision.
   - A pairwise blind judge, v2 versus unedited, with ties when identical.
   - Owner accept/reject per edit, where available.
   - **Harm bound** (one-sided exact Poisson, zero harmful edits observed): per 100 sentences, 1.15 at 10 drafts (design effect 4), 0.58 at 20, 0.29 at 40.
   - **Net benefit** = improving − λ × harmful, with λ=5 as a placeholder for the owner's priority order.
3. **Idempotence.** v2 on its own output and on already-processed text should give near-zero edits, with the voice lock holding.
   - [M] Baseline from 3 Oct: 17/20 already-processed posts were changed again (about 0.4–0.7% character edit). That was the text-file route.
   - [R] Planning figure: P(unchanged) 0.15 → 0.40 needs 26 posts (80% power, φ=0.3).
4. **Cost.** v2 must not exceed the old engine's calls. When nothing is detected it should make 0 or 1 call, not 2 calls and 0 edits as the old engine did in 8/10.

**Draft→published pairs.**
- For the 10 new articles, original = published, so the "old engine's real output" is the identity. I recommend archiving every future Hermes `body-original.txt` as the corpus and logging drafts in shadow mode.
- If pairs with genuine old-engine edits are supplied (older posts or the text-file route), run three outputs per draft: the historical published text P, an old-engine re-run O, and v2 V.
  - Offline, with no model cost: per-sentence voice drift of P against the draft, which is the old engine's real 말투 drift rate.
  - P versus O measures old-arm run variance.
  - V versus P: blind pairwise preference, edit-level adjudication, meaning and CTA against the draft. The caveat is that P may include manual post-edits.
  - Power, clustered by post (units = paragraphs):

    | Target | Posts needed |
    |---|---|
    | Preference, win rate 0.65 among non-tied | 40–66 posts |
    | Preference, win rate 0.60 | 91–149 posts |
    | Voice NI (3 pp) on paragraphs | 195–320 posts |

    So with fewer than about 40 pairs only descriptive claims hold.
- If drafts are unavailable:
  - rely on the spiked-draft study built from whatever drafts exist, the seed set, and the private round-2 stratum;
  - add prospective shadow-mode logging with an owner blind A/B pick.

## 4. Metrics

Per item per arm unless noted. Tier-1 computation is deterministic (`src/score.py`). Sentence alignment is monotone DP on character-bigram Dice.

| Metric | How computed |
|---|---|
| **Awkwardness repair** | Per injected span: FIXED_EXACT (aligned sentence equals gold or an acceptable fix), FIXED_PROBABLE (`defect_regex` absent and Dice to gold ≥0.6), REWRITTEN_ADJUDICATE (judge), UNFIXED (defect regex still matches), DELETED. Only "primary" injections count: high confidence, not genre-dependent, not an LLM tell, and the gold fix leaves the sentence's voice features unchanged. |
| **Over-editing** | Any-edit rate, clean-sentence touch rate (sentences with no injection), protected-token changes, harm judged by the audit. In the default configuration a changed colloquial token or CMC token is harm. |
| **Voice** | Per aligned sentence, features: level, ending class (about 20 classes such as 더라고요/거든요/잖아요/네요/죠/ㄹ게요/합쇼 declarative), honorific multiset (Kiwi `-시-`, 께서, 님, lexical), humble forms, first-person markers. A violation is any difference; quotations must stay verbatim. Sentences whose gold fix itself changes voice (7 HONOR items) are exempt and reported as their own stratum, with the fix-or-leave decision left to the owner. No document register is used. |
| **Meaning** | Tiers: (1) deterministic invariants, with numbers exact and negation, modality, quantifier, condition, role regexes (lenient); (2) frozen independent audit with a Kiwi polarity and modality extractor, never imported by an arm; (3) blinded bidirectional-entailment LLM judge (two judges, order swapped, a different family from the writer); (4) human audit of a stratified subset plus every tier disagreement. The engine's own guards are reported separately as "engine-visible", not as an outcome. The probes calibrate (2) and (3); if their sensitivity or specificity is poor, rates are corrected (Rogan–Gladen). |
| **CTA** | Ledger per CTA with action, object, force (1–4), condition, channel, deadline. Preserved means present with force not lowered and specificity kept. Also: new CTA sentences in CTA-free items, and advice sentences scored separately from explicit CTAs. |
| **Quality (clarity, flow, CTA)** | Blinded pairwise LLM judge, both orders, two judges, rubric (the T4/T5/T6 anchors). Item score = average over orders, ties 0.5; identical outputs are not judged. Calibrate on 120 human-rated pairs, two raters. Acceptance rules [R]: κ ≥ 0.6 and swap-consistency ≥ 80%, else the judge result is exploratory only. |
| **Cost** | Calls, retries, rollbacks, unusable outputs, gate codes, prepare/codex/gate ms and total ms, prompt chars, and completion or error rate. |

**Primary endpoint** (fixed sequence, only if all four safety gates pass): clean repair rate on primary injections, paired v2 minus old, two-sided α=0.05.

**Safety co-primaries** (intersection-union, each one-sided α=0.025, paired, absolute margins on harm rate):

| Gate | Endpoint | Margin δ | Basis | Required N |
|---|---|---:|---|---:|
| S1 | meaning change per item | 3 pp | base ≤5%, φ=0.3 | 695 |
| S2 | voice violation per item | 3 pp | same | 695 |
| S3 | explicit CTA degradation per CTA-bearing item | 5 pp | base 5%, φ=0.3 | 277 |
| S4 | harm per no-edit-correct item | 5 pp | same | 277 |
| S5 | real drafts: harmful edits per 100 sentences, upper bound below 1.0 | – | exact Poisson | see 3.5 |

- S3 needs at least 280 CTA-bearing items, which the plan provides (section 3.4).
- The margins are [R] assumptions that the owner should confirm, not measured quantities.

**Secondary endpoints** are Holm-adjusted: three judge win rates, voice superiority, meaning superiority, edit rate on controls.

**Descriptive endpoints** (95% CI only): cost, latency ratio (NI margin 1.25 reported but not gating), calls, rollbacks.

**Exploratory endpoints:** per-stratum effects use an interaction test and Benjamini–Hochberg q=0.10. They are never reported as per-stratum significance.

## 8. Harness design (not run)

- **Isolation:**
  - `git archive <rev> | tar -x -C $BENCH/arms/<arm>` for c792440, 4b6a7a9 and the v2 commit.
  - Run with `tsx` from the arm directory, or a built copy inside it, never from the main tree and never `npm run test:e2e`. The Python stages run from the checkout and `dist/` is executed by the hourly watchdog, so the main tree must stay untouched.
  - Do not call port 7882. Use a temp `runtimeRoot` under `$BENCH`.
  - `scripts/benchmark-speed.mjs` is the template (`BENCH_DIST`, `BENCH_PROVIDER`, `BENCH_MODEL`).
- **Runner:**
  - One job at a time. Order alternates by item hash, with interleaved blocks to spread provider drift.
  - Resumable, with append and fsync per job, keyed by (item, arm, rep).
  - Fixed route options. For each job log `SUPER_SEJONG_MODE`, route (direct-text/medium or text-file/normal), model, effort, arm sha, a hash of rulebook and prompt files, and the sha256 of input and output.
  - Per job log also: `TransformDiagnostics` (calls, retries, rollbacks, gate codes, hard and shadow failure counts, style findings before and after, repaired particles, timings), warning codes, prompt chars, error code.
- **Blinding:**
  - A separate packer strips arm identity, randomises A/B with a recorded seed kept in a sealed key file, and builds the judge packets.
  - Judges never see arm names or gate flags. Analysis joins afterwards.
- **Keeping model text out of the repo:**
  - Outputs and judge packets live in `$BENCH/private/` (git-ignored, outside the repo). The repo gets only JSONL metrics with ids, hashes, counts and labels.
  - For real drafts, never store bodies. Use only hashes, offsets and aggregates, as in the 3 Oct report (`rawTextIncluded:false`).
  - The seed set may live in the repo because it is original text. Model outputs must not, because their licence and content are unclear.
- **Offline-safe modes:**
  - Tier A and B need no network and no CLI.
  - Tier C needs the authenticated CLI, run by the owner.
  - Judge calls go to a different provider from the writer, in parallel.
- **Analysis code** (`score.py`, `voicefeat.py`) imports no engine code. It is frozen and hash-pinned before the sealed run. Kiwi 0.24.0 (Apache-2.0) is used only offline, as the repo evaluation already allows.

## 9. What the 윤문 엔진 gets wrong or misses

All [M] unless marked.

- On the owner's genre it makes 0 edits in 10/10 (463 of 640 sentences protected by a blanket regex) and spends a retry and a second model call in 8/10 (lead's fact). The 3 Oct benchmark's 17/20 changes came from a different route.
- Its protection is trigger-based, not edit-based: 224 sentences are protected by modal/negation/capability words, 182 by condition/cause words, 126 by digits or quotes. A meaning guard checked per edit would allow safe edits in these sentences.
- A random defect sits in a protected sentence 56–100% of the time, so its repair ceiling there is about zero. Its particle repair acts only on rewrite-introduced mismatches.
- No voice gate: a 합쇼체 document with 19% 해요체 sentences is mixed by design, and nothing guards per-sentence level, honorifics, ending style or first-person markers.
- No CTA ledger: the owner's drafts carry about 8 imperative/request sentences each.
- The v1 diagnostics recall 12.7% of injected defects and the v1 meaning guards miss role swaps (0/5), temporal flips (3/4 missed) and unit changes.
- Not idempotent on the text-file route (3/20 unchanged on a second pass).

## 10. Recommendations for v2

1. Replace blanket protection with per-edit guards (event-based meaning check plus per-sentence voice and CTA checks), so protected sentences can be edited when the guard passes.
2. Deterministic repair of certain source errors (allomorph, double passive, spelling spacing) without a model call. Skip the model call entirely when nothing is detected: 0 or 1 call, never a retry on unchanged text.
3. Per-sentence voice lock built on the same feature set as the scorer (but a different implementation for evaluation), and a CTA ledger.
4. Protected-token and quotation guards for CMC tokens and colloquial spellings, with the opt-in off by default.
5. Run the seed set as the internal pilot, then lock N. Confirm margins and λ with the owner before the sealed run.
